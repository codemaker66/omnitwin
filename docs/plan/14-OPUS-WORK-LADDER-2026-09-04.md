## 1. Decided by the Fable session, not to be re-derived

- The blur on the floor is content (794,351 translucent 2 cm discs), so the floor needs another source; no renderer setting fixes it.
- Every XGRIDS level is the whole room at one density; serving is one level plus the sky, never a stack; the coarsest level is the first view.

- Bright Walls is training evidence once registered, not a second splat to blend. Panoramas enter as pinhole crops, never as panoramas.
- The room's floor is the served Gaussians' densest slab (T-578, done); the manifest is regenerated by `lcc2 stage` then `lcc2 lod`.
