## 5. Laws that do not move

Any change that breaks one of these is wrong even if it looks better.

1. **The instrument is not edited.** `craft-quiz-model.ts` and `craft-quiz-deliberation.ts` keep every vector, weight and threshold. No module under `stage/ lantern/ react/ audio/ ceremony/ state/` may import them; source-grep tests enforce it.
2. **No scroll, anywhere, at any viewport.** The stage is `position: fixed; inset: 0`; the narrative sits in a wrapper that is `height: 100dvh; overflow: clip` — never on `body`. Judge: `expectNoPageScroll` in the existing responsive e2e, which measures `max(documentElement, body).scrollHeight` against `clientHeight`.
3. **Springs, never tweens**, for anything that moves in response to input — `lib/springs.ts`, stepped in a rAF loop that parks on `isSpringSettled`. No CSS `transition` or `animation` on `transform`; no `filter` on anything over a quarter of the viewport.
4. **Reduced motion never freezes pointer-following.** Every beat keeps its timestamp; transforms become opacity or colour; the visible "Still" switch overrides the OS setting either way.
5. **DOM narrative first.** Canvas layers are `aria-hidden`; every pokeable is a real `<button>` with a layperson's name; every cue has a caption; exactly one `aria-live="polite"` region.
6. **The fairness lint.** No prop label, caption, aside, cue id, cue file name or plane id may carry a `WORLD_LEXICON` word for the scene's four worlds unless the shipped scene text already carries it, and never a Craft name. Scene 1's worlds are hammermen, gardeners, tailors and cordiners — so "iron", "seed", "cloth", "boots" and their siblings are banned; "chain", "lantern", "toll box", "swallow", "water", "jetty", "port" are fine. The list is in `features/trades-house/__tests__/world-lexicon.ts`.
7. **Captions mirror, never grade.** No praise, no blame, no "brave" or "wise".
8. **Rarity is deterministic.** No `Math.random()`, no `Date.now()` in the reducers or stores — the clock is injected. Everyone gets the same room.
9. **TypeScript strict, zero `any`**, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, `verbatimModuleSyntax`; ESLint `strictTypeChecked`; relative imports end in `.js`. No suppressions, no TODOs.

## 7. Provenance rules for anything new you generate

Every shipped asset carries a card: the tool and its version, the exact prompt, the licence, the date, and who ran it. The schema is `ProvenanceSchema` in `stage/stage-manifest.ts`; the gate fails the build on a missing card. Source files are never deleted. What is still forbidden, override or not: frame interpolation, model upscaling or denoising, generative fill, and style transfer into a drawn asset — because each of those makes a pixel nobody can account for.
