## The one law that decides what is allowed (measured)

The sorting must never be readable from the screen before the reveal (the instrument's law; brief 17's X7 "telegraphy" and X8 "the explained moral"; plan §8). A transformation is a signal. Which signals are safe?

- **The Craft tally (the world channel) — NEVER.** Any state keyed on which Craft leads is the Q7-omen leak the plan just deleted.
- **A temperament axis — only if both poles are wide.** Measured on the shipped `CRAFT_AXIS_PROFILES` (2026-09-04): a1 Lasting↔Living +7/−5; a2 Perfection↔Provision +9/−4; a3 Bench↔Hearth +7/−5; a4 Bold↔Steady **+4/−8**; a5 Make↔Keep +6/−6. A state keyed to the Bold pole names four Crafts (Hammermen, Tailors, Dyers, Fleshers); keyed to Provision, four (Cordiners, Bakers, Fleshers, Maltmen). So a2 and a4 may never drive a visible state; a1, a3 and a5 may, only symmetrically (both poles get an equally rich, equally beautiful state, and nothing accumulates toward "more of one pole = better"), and never more than one axis at a time, because two axes together narrow the field to a handful.
- **The consequence of a specific choice — YES, and it is the strongest material.** "You left the fence as it was" / "you went into the river" / "you took down the gate" is what the reader already knows they did; showing its consequence later in the burgh, on the page and on the narrator is Dostoevsky's witness-who-remembers (brief 17 L3, X4) and the plan already does it in miniature (memories keyed on an earlier scene's world; the ledger; the persistence table in §5). It must mirror, never grade: X6 (the moralising narrator) and X8 (post-choice characterisation) still bind. The town can burn because you went in; the narrator can carry the soot; nobody says it was brave.
- **The wear of the night — YES.** Time passes in the Hall: the candle burns down, the Convener takes off the chain, the window's colour turns toward dawn, the book fills. This is state that changes with progress and reads as care, and it grades nothing.

Fable's horns-and-halo (alignment made visible as virtue) is exactly what X6/X8 forbid and it would also be the instrument's leak by another name. Baldur's Gate's *world remembering what you did* is the model.

## What you must decide or design (be concrete; numbers; provenance)

1. The **consequence system**: which choices leave which marks, where (later tableaux, the page's document frame, the Convener's frame and person, the Hall at the ceremonies, the reveal's "town you made"), how they compound, and how they stay story-true. Every consequence names the option it follows from.
2. The **narrator's transformation**: what changes on the man (dress, chain, candle, soot, frost, ink, tiredness, the hour), what changes in his room, what he says differently — all as consequences or as the night's wear, never as verdicts. How the filmed skin delivers it (state layers, filmed variants, camera moves on the frame, composited practicals) without an explosion of clips; what the painting fallback can do.
3. The **quiz's own state**: the page on fire, rusted, frozen, flooded, blooming, worn, gilded — which states exist, what triggers each, how they are drawn (Lantern, layers, filmed practicals, procedural), how many can coexist, and how they resolve so the no-scroll law, the fairness lint and the phone budget survive.
4. **Asset explosion control**: twelve scenes × states must not become hundreds of paintings. State the combinatorics honestly and the technique that keeps it bounded (layered states, masks, procedural, filmed practicals, one painting with N overlays, etc.).
5. **Tooling to ask Blake for**, by name, with what each buys: generative image/video APIs (and where their output is allowed under plan §7's provenance gates — declared, scrutinised, never a shipped hero pixel unless it survives the lineup), Blender pipelines, a foley library, a motion-capture or rotoscope tool, an actor day, Blake's own time.
6. **Fairness proof**: a written argument, per state, that it cannot narrow the field; a lint that can check it from the manifests; the experiment addition that would detect a leak on real runs.
7. **Story sense**: if the reactive layer needs the story rebuilt, rebuild it (permission given) — but the twelve scenes' worlds and vectors stay, the memories stay keyed, and the voice rules of §3 hold. Show at least two full scene passages in the new voice that demonstrate a consequence arriving.
8. **Delivery**: cards in the plan's format (one session, done-when, verify, forbidden), slotted into the existing order, with costs.
