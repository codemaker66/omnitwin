## Art direction for The River Gate (the tableau is drawn by code)
Composition, desktop 16:9, with a 9:16 phone crop that keeps the port, the lantern and the figure in frame: the horizon at 58% of the height; the river is the lower 42%. Along the horizon, a low dark silhouette of a Lowland burgh on the far bank: crow-stepped gables, a tolbooth steeple, a squat kirk tower, chimneys; the Water Port a small dark arch at the waterline, low and centre; ONE lantern on a bracket beside the port, the only saturated hue in the frame (amber near #e39b3a). Fourteen to twenty tiny warm windows along the skyline (each a StageLight for the water). At the bottom left a foreground plane: the near bank and a jetty with a mooring post, the port chain hanging into the water from the post, the toll box on the jetty; the traveller with a bag on a stick, a silhouette one twentieth of the frame's height, standing on the jetty looking across ("a small figure against a thing too large for the frame"). A swallow crossing high; an apple core drifting by the jetty.

Sky: Lammas dusk after sunset: deep indigo (#0c1020) at the top through slate to a thin band of dying ember (#5a2a12 to #b0552a) at the horizon; thin cloud streaks from layered noise; the first stars fading in over the scene's minutes (the wear of the night, `dusk` 0 to 1). No moon.

Water: near-black; the horizon's ember stretched into a vertical shimmer; the lantern's reflection a broken amber column; the windows tiny broken streaks; a slow swell; expanding rings from a poke (at most 8); a low mist band over the far water.

Light: 90% of the frame near-black and earth; the lantern and its halo on the port wall are the light; the pointer light warms what it passes by 4%.

Grammar: Turner and John Martin: vastness, obscurity, one practical light. Nothing that reads as a game UI, clip-art, a template or a stock illustration: no glowing text, no knotwork, no tartan, no drop shadows, no lens flares. Motion is slow and physical; nothing loops visibly. Silhouettes have varied, irregular rooflines and hand-drawn edges, never repeated modules.

The document frame (prospect-map genre): the tableau sits inside a paper margin with a hand-ruled border, a small cartouche "The River Gate. Lammas." in the quiz's serif (Cormorant Garamond and EB Garamond are already loaded by `TradesHouseCraftQuizPage.css`), a compass rose in a corner, and the ledger strip along the left margin: twelve faint slots, and after the commit one gold stroke draws itself by `stroke-dashoffset` under a spring scalar. On phones the margin becomes a 6 to 8 px paper edge and the cartouche sits at the top.

The narrative on the stage: desktop keeps the Convener's frame (the existing `ConvenerPainting` skin, unchanged) at the lower left with his bubble, and the four options as a column on a translucent paper panel at the right; on phones the tableau fills the screen and the options are a two-by-two grid of leads on a paper panel at the bottom, with the held card on tap. Everything stays inside 100dvh at every viewport in the existing e2e's list.

## Interaction (spec section 5)
Scene 1's pokeables, as manifest props with boxes in percent of the stage: the port chain (swings on `camera` 120/14 and stays swinging when you come back; `persistKey: "chainSwing"`), the water (a ring and a plunk), the lantern (gutters; the flame spring rights it), the swallow (leaves; returns once, on `distinctProps:3`), the toll box (four rows: the lid, a coin, coins, then a bolt slides), the apple core (turns; on the fourth poke it sinks). Pokeables are inert while the scene line plays (the caption is "(he is still speaking)"). Anticipation on fine pointers only: a 2 px lift on 120/14. One spring, one cue and one caption per poke, in the same rAF frame; captions in square brackets, at most 140 characters, mirroring what happened, never grading; no lexicon words.

The choice ceremony: desktop one tap commits; phone lifts the option into the held card (lead, body, cost; the cost read through the live region) and a second tap commits; Escape or tap-away puts it back. t0 press: `scale(0.97)` 100 ms and the paper-touch cue at -18 dB. t1 commit: the seal strikes on `strike` with its cue on the same frame and a firm haptic (Android only); the glow on a `::after` opacity, never a box-shadow. t1 + 40 ms: the three unchosen cards translate toward the chosen one (inward, never outward) and dim to 0.42 on 120/14, staggered 40 ms; on phones they leave the visual stack but stay in the DOM as `visibility: hidden; height: 0` with `tabindex="-1"` set only after focus has moved to Continue. t1 + 120 ms: the reply panel, pre-mounted `visibility: hidden` and `inert`, rises 8 px on `gridSettle`; the Convener's reply typewriter runs on the audio clock as today. After the reply, the ledger stroke draws. Then the latch line in its own slot (the manifest's `latch`, at most 14 words). Continue plays the page-turn and hands off to scene 2 in the existing skin. Keyboard: Enter or Space is the tap (phone: first opens, second commits); arrows move between options; Escape closes; the "In the room" group is one Tab stop with a roving tabindex (Left, Right, Home, End inside it); M mutes; C toggles captions.
