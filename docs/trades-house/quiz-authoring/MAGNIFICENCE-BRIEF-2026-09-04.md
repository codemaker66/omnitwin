## 3. The instrument (do not break it; it is the whole reason the quiz is fair)

- Sorting = two channels: five bipolar temperament axes (A1 Lasting↔Living, A2 Perfection↔Provision, A3 Bench↔Hearth, A4 Bold↔Steady, A5 Make↔Keep) scored by cosine against 14 fixed Craft directions, PLUS a "world" channel (each option lives in one Craft's material world; score adds 0.9 × share of that Craft's homes chosen, normalised). Measured fair: a seeded 20,000-respondent dice population lands every Craft between 4% and 11%; consistent respondents 4.6–9.2%.
- Invariants held by tests (`craft-quiz-reachability.test.ts`, `craft-quiz-deliberation.test.ts`): four DISTINCT worlds per scene; 3–4 home answers per Craft; a home answer's temperament vector within cos 0.55 of its Craft; every home answer contains ≥1 word from that Craft's world-lexicon (lay legibility); no Craft named; sentences ≤30 words; fairness band 4–11%; the deliberation "actually decides" both ways.
- The dangerously close pairs: Masons–Weavers (0.79), Hammermen–Tailors (0.77), Bakers–Maltmen (0.75), Masons–Wrights, Coopers–Skinners. Twelve authored deliberation scenes exist for the pairs that finish close most often.
- THE FINDING THAT MATTERS FOR THIS BRIEF (`docs/trades-house/quiz-authoring/FORMAT-RESEARCH-2026-08-16.md`): the answer FORMAT is at its psychometric ceiling (changing it moves ≤6 points). But respondents who answer NOISILY lose ~20 points of top-1 accuracy versus consistent ones — and noise is attention. Engagement is therefore not decoration on this instrument; it is its largest remaining lever. ~13% of respondents have no fact of the matter (two Crafts within 0.03) and the honest instrument says so ("it hung between two").
- Real data: `quiz_runs` now records twelve seat indices + verdict per completed run (no PII). Consumers must recompute the verdict from `answers`.

1. Fairness: every Craft equally achievable; all invariants in §3 kept or strengthened; every new scene passes the same tests before it ships.
2. 3. Nothing scrolls, any screen, any viewport, phone to 3440×1440.
4. No PII; DNT/GPC honoured; no dark patterns (no fake scarcity, no fake variable reward, no manipulation of a 400-year-old institution's members).
5. Accessible: keyboard-complete, screen-reader coherent (a DOM narrative exists under any canvas), reduced-motion honoured (rhythm kept, motion removed), captions for all audio, sound opt-in and never autoplaying without a gesture.
6. Performance: a phone on 4G must reach the first scene in seconds, not tens of seconds; 60 fps in motion on mid laptops; poster fallback for weak devices; budgets stated as numbers.
7. "If anything looks like AI people will not use it." Every visual element's provenance must be stated: captured, photographed, public-domain period art, commissioned, procedurally generated from real sources, or hand-made in Blender. Generative-AI imagery is not banned outright but anything used must be indistinguishable from directed work under scrutiny and must be declared in the plan.
8. Voice: the Convener's lines are spoken; ElevenLabs credits are finite; a real actor is on the table if the plan argues for it.
9. Everything ships through the repo's gates (typecheck, lint, tests, e2e, handoff protocol) in cards small enough for one session each.
