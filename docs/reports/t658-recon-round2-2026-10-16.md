## Outcome

- **The instrument is fixed.** A kept control (22.527 dB; a replica of the same recipe scored 22.546, so two runs agree
  to 0.02 dB), masks for the surfaces that carry the error, range-relative geometry and two sealed folds.
- **No training lever moves the held-out stations by the 0.10 dB the rules require.** LiDAR line-of-sight supervision
  and per-light-state view-dependent colour hold; a line-of-sight variant sparing the glossy surfaces was predicted at
  -0.002 dB and not run.
- **One rendering change pays where the viewer draws the hall small.** The native viewer's existing anti-aliasing
  compensation, off for captured splats since T-639, gains +0.54 dB at quarter scale and +0.08 dB at half scale for
  0.01 dB at full scale. Training in the compensated raster with a re-implemented Mip-Splatting filter promotes on its
  judge, but adds only +0.03 dB of its own.
- **The next gains come from the site day:** HDR brackets for the windows and lamps, and more viewpoints near the
  glossy walls.

## What this round does

The panorama splat of the Grand Hall is scored on five held-out stations (fold A: sweeps 7, 22, 33, 36 and 43, six
faces each at 4096 px). Before spending GPU on new levers, this round fixes the measuring instrument, reads cheap
oracles that bound what each lever could win, and only then runs the levers the oracles allow, under rules written
before any run.

## Pre-registered rules (written 11:23 UTC, before any pod)

- **Screening on fold A:** promote at +0.10 dB or more on the headline when 4 of 5 sweeps improve, the raw render
  gains at least 0.05 dB and no region holding at least 1% of the scored pixels falls by more than 0.20 dB; kill at
  -0.10 dB; otherwise hold. Statistics are clustered by sweep.
- **Adoption on sealed folds:** a mean gain of +0.10 dB or more over 10 sweeps, cluster t of at least 2.262 on 9 degrees
  of freedom, and 8 of 10 sweeps better.
- **Sealed folds**, drawn by a fixed procedure (stratified by light state, hall thirds and station spacing, seed
  20261016) and checked by an independent second computation: **B = sweeps 1, 14, 24, 31, 39; C = 6, 20, 26, 34, 42.**
  No view from them is scored until a lever is up for adoption.
- **Oracle gates:** local exposure needs +0.15 dB from a per-face 16×16 log-gain field; LiDAR line-of-sight
  supervision needs +0.30 dB from excluding floater and hole pixels, or most floater-causing Gaussians in LiDAR-certified
  free space; a pose arm needs +0.30 dB from held-out pose refinement; HDR capture is recommended if clipped and window
  pixels carry 15% or more of the upper wall's error.

## The floor-reflection layer is held

## The oracles

| Oracle | Result | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 16×16 local gain per face | +1.17 dB over matched | Mostly the test photos' own tone mapping (the field is fitted to each test photo), not a lever; a per-sweep bilateral grid gained nothing held-out on 3 October |
| Floater and hole pixels excluded | +0.39 dB | Line-of-sight supervision runs |
| Gaussian-ID trace | 67% of the floater-causing Gaussians' weight lies in free space certified by 2+ training stations | Agrees |
| Held-out pose refinement | +0.01 dB (corrections under 0.2 mrad, 1.1 mm) | Registration is not the limit; no pose arm |
| Clipped and window share of upper-wall error | 20% and 18% | **HDR bracket capture recommended** for the windows and lamps: no model can recover clipped light |

The upper-wall lever the masks point to is **per-light-state view-dependent colour**: the trainer gives each light state
its own base colour but shares spherical-harmonic bands 1–3, so the same glass and varnish must show daylight by day
and the lit interior's reflections at night. Giving the evening its own bands ships as is, because the viewer already
loads one splat per light state.

## The levers

Each lever ran once on fold A against the control under the screening rule, or was decided on its predicted effect
before any money was spent.

| Lever | Fold A, against the control | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| LiDAR line-of-sight supervision | -0.070 dB (1 of 5 sweeps better), raw -0.080 dB; range-relative holes halved (4.03% to 2.11%), floaters unchanged; portraits -0.47 dB | Hold |
| Per-light-state view-dependent colour | -0.023 dB (3 of 5), raw -0.015 dB; at night honours boards +0.34 dB and portraits +0.06, the ceiling -0.22 and the lower wall -0.20 | Hold |
| Line of sight sparing portraits and honours boards | Predicted -0.002 dB from fold A's per-class sums | Not run |
| Anti-aliased training (gsplat's compensated raster plus a re-implemented Mip-Splatting 3D filter) | +0.570 dB at quarter scale against today's rendering (5 of 5, t(4) 5.29), +0.037 dB at full scale; its own share over the compensation switch +0.030 and +0.018 dB | Promote; sealed folds not spent (below) |
| Confidence-aware densification at 1.0M and 1.5M splats | Predicted +0.25 and +0.15 dB over plain MCMC at the same native budget | Built and smoke-tested, not launched |

- **Line of sight** did to the geometry what it was built for, and cost the pictures: pulling weight back to the LiDAR
  surface removed the virtual depth 3DGS uses to show the varnish's reflections (Gaussians 0.3-1 m behind the
  portraits fell from 4,913 to 1,836), as predicted before the run.
- **Per-state colour** fixed what it targeted at night, but the evening's own bands, fitted from the evening views alone,
  overfit plain surfaces. Restricted to the glossy surfaces it would add +0.01 dB.
- **Anti-aliased training** promotes because of the viewer switch below; the training itself adds +0.03 dB at quarter
  scale and +0.02 dB at full scale, inside the 0.02 dB run-to-run noise. The sealed folds would confirm the switch's
  gain (already t(4) 6.3 on five sweeps), not the training's share, which ten sweeps cannot resolve, so they stay
  unspent unless asked for. The training flags cost nothing to add to the next final, and they remove the switch's
  0.01 dB full-scale cost.
- **Confidence-aware densification** (arXiv 2610.13541, re-implemented inside the trainer's MCMC; the paper publishes
  no code) improves a model trained at a phone budget, but phones receive the 1.5M level of the full model's level
  tree, not a natively trained model, so no viewer would see the gain. The decisive phone experiment comes first: a
  natively trained 1.5M model against the shipped 1.5M level.

## The viewer's anti-aliasing switch

The native viewer draws captured splats without its anti-aliasing compensation (T-639 turned it off as false
dimming). Measured on the control replica, nothing retrained:

| Scale (face size) | Compensation off | On | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full (4096 px) | 22.546 dB | 22.532 dB | -0.014 dB (0 of 5 sweeps better) |
| Half (2048 px) | 22.812 dB | 22.887 dB | +0.075 dB (5 of 5) |
| Quarter (1024 px) | 22.958 dB | 23.497 dB | +0.539 dB (5 of 5, t(4) 6.33) |

At quarter scale every region gains, most on fine ornament: the frieze +1.8 dB, the 2 px around edges +1.4, the
ceiling +1.0, the chandeliers +0.8. The classic raster widens every splat that shrinks below a pixel, so zoomed-out
views (the plan view, a phone at reduced resolution) over-cover fine detail; the compensation keeps each splat's
energy. Turning it on costs 0.014 dB at the photographs' own scale and gains at every smaller one. It is a viewer
setting and Blake's call.

## Model-side levers have stopped paying at this capture

Every training lever that ships with today's viewer has now been measured or bounded on the corrected instrument, and
none moves the held-out stations by the 0.10 dB the rules require. What remains sits where the photographs carry
information the model cannot recover: glare on varnished portraits and honours boards seen from angles no station
saw, light clipped in windows and bulbs, and the view through the glass. The next gains come from the site day, not
from further model levers on this capture:

- **HDR brackets** at the stations below, for the windows and lamps (clipped pixels and windows carry 20% and 18% of
  the upper wall's error);
- **more viewpoints near the glossy walls:** extra stations along the portrait and honours-board walls, or XGRIDS walk
  frames between stations, so the varnish's glare is seen from more angles.

The sealed folds B and C stay unscored for whatever that capture produces.
