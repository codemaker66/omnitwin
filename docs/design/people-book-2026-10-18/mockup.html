Every name, club, date and figure in the picture is illustrative. The layout follows the Enquiries desk you called "not a slog to the eyes, it is inviting".

What it replaces in Salesforce

  • Accounts, Contacts and Leads, as one record per person and per organisation or household
  • Tasks, Events and Notes, as one timeline built from the real records
  • Duplicate rules, as suggestions on both records and a merge you can undo for 30 days
  • The Data Export, as a repeatable import that never duplicates, until Salesforce can go

Five pieces, in order

  1. The record: people, organisations and households you can edit and merge; sales can finally use it
  2. This desk and the timeline, with calls logged in under fifteen seconds
  3. Consent, the two-year deletion the privacy policy promises, export and erasure
  4. The Salesforce import: read in your browser, dry run, apply, roll back
  5. Who's who on each event, and "same again"

Calls I made, for you to check

  • The existing client and contact records are improved in place, so no history is lost
  • Records join by themselves only on an exact email; everything else is a suggestion
  • Deletion after two years runs by itself once an admin has confirmed its first run
  • Export and erasure are for admins and managers, with exactly what goes shown first
  • Hallkeepers see only the day-of contact for their own events
  • Guests never enter the book: they stay with their own event

What Trades House needs to send

  • Who owns which client relationships
