# Trades Hall facade — img2threejs image analysis

Intended use: a static, presentation-only, real-time browser facade stand-in. It is not measured geometry and must not become Scene Authority or historical-capture evidence.

## 1. Identification and classification

- Primary type: symmetrical neoclassical urban building facade, confidence 0.96.
- Primary domain: object.
- Form language: architectural hard-surface, layered shell, repeated modular bays.
- Motion potential: static whole-object transform; optional clickable/explodable presentation groups only.

## 2. Overall form and silhouette

- Bounding volume: a wide shallow cuboid with stepped pavilion/parapet masses and a central raised dome volume.
- Symmetry: strongly bilateral about the central entrance/portico axis.
- Observable proportions: width is about 1.55 times the visible stone facade height; the dome adds roughly one third of that height above the central parapet.
- Geometry strategy: assembled cuboids for the wall tiers; extruded profiles for pediments and arches; cylinders for columns; lathed/revolved dome and cupola; instanced repeated window, medallion and baluster modules.

## 4. Spatial relationships

- The rusticated tier supports the smoother upper facade and is flush with a shallow rear building block.
- The entrance is embedded in the centre of the rusticated tier.
- The portico overlaps the central upper wall and projects in front of it; its columns butt into bases and support the pediment/entablature.
- The three arched window assemblies are embedded in the middle tier, one on each side and one behind the portico.
- Pavilion stacks sit above the outer wings; balustrades bridge pavilion and central parapet masses.
- The drum is attached above the central parapet; the dome is socketed on the drum; the cupola is socketed on the dome crown.

## 5. Materials and PBR response

- Aged buff sandstone: dielectric, roughness 0.68–0.84, low-frequency albedo variation, darker cavity/AO masks.
- Dark window glazing: dielectric, roughness 0.18–0.32, restrained environment reflection; no transparent interior is required.
- Painted timber frames/doors: dielectric, roughness 0.42–0.62.
- Oxidised copper dome: metalness 0.9–1.0, roughness 0.48–0.68, green/blue patina variation.
- Small dark metalwork: metalness 0.75–0.95, roughness 0.4–0.6.

## 6. Colour and finish

- Stone: low-saturation warm beige, medium-high value, locally darker at the plinth, joints and ledges.
- Glazing/cavities: low-value blue-black to charcoal.
- Timber: low-value neutral to warm brown.
- Copper: muted blue-green, medium value, with darker ribs and weathering.

## 8. Uncertainty and single-image limits

- The reference is a locally decoded and tightly cropped copy of the repository's frontal facade photograph. It is visual evidence, not a metric survey.
- Side and rear elevations are hidden by the frontal view. The model must remain a shallow facade volume and should not claim a complete building reconstruction.
- Exact dimensions, depth, stone-joint spacing, flagpole geometry, crest sculpture and relief iconography are undetermined.
- Adjacent buildings and street furniture are outside the target. Only the clearly visible Trades Hall frontage controls bay count and silhouette during review.
- Fine statues, heraldry and text are intentionally reduced to abstract relief masses at real-time scale.

## Suitability verdict

Conditional pass for a stylised presentation-only facade object. It has one clear target, a strong silhouette and reconstructable hard-surface modules. It is unsuitable for survey, collision, planning dimensions or a complete four-sided building claim.
