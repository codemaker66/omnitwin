# Hallkeeper experience: design acceptance

7 September 2026 · T-606 expanded design objective · v2 reviewed

## Scope and standard

The requested outcome is a deeply considered hallkeeper workflow and a polished, explorable concept: model the judgement of a hallkeeper with 50 years of experience; make receiving and interpreting layouts, office coordination, client/booker care and issue resolution simple; improve the prospect of productive, enjoyable work; attend to the details; and preserve the supplied visual direction. The original request also calls for internet research and prompts for images Blake will generate.

**“100/100” is the ambition, not a measured score or a claim of universal perfection.** Completion means the requested experience is coherently designed, demonstrated and reviewed against the gates below. Blake remains the aesthetic judge. Staff interviews, operational trials and measured productivity improvements are later validation of the design hypotheses; their absence must be stated, but does not prevent completion of this requested design deliverable.

The concept uses fictional records and explicit simulated interactions. It is not a production implementation. The current request does not require an unrelated rollout, live supplier messaging, database mutations or other application owners' work to be completed by this design lane. A working simulation may demonstrate an accepted handover or client response only when it remains unmistakably a simulation.

## Minimum explorable scenarios

These are scenario gates for the v2 concept, not requirements to implement every backend capability or every future screen. A scenario can span several views of one consistent fictional event. The concept should make success and at least one meaningful recovery leg understandable through actual interaction.

| Scenario | Starting situation → required demonstration | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|
| S1 · Receive the arrangement | Nora selects a room → opens its issued plan → locates an important instruction → asks for clarification or acknowledges receipt → returns to the same room and work context. | **Demonstrated** — S1 outcome in [browser evidence](verification-v2.md#browser-scenarios) and [passing assertions](../../../output/playwright/hallkeeper-care-2026-09-07/final-scenarios.json). |
| S2 · A changed promise | The organiser asks for more guests → Nora captures the request → the office accepts the decision → consequences and a next update are clear → Nora sees what can continue → an issued answer changes only the applicable work. | **Demonstrated** — S2 outcome in [browser evidence](verification-v2.md#browser-scenarios) and [passing assertions](../../../output/playwright/hallkeeper-care-2026-09-07/final-scenarios.json). |
| S3 · Care without exposing the back office | Nora prepares to welcome the organiser → sees the agreed personal priority and contact → opens the organiser glance view → gives a truthful update → returns privately to work. | **Demonstrated** — S3 outcome in [browser evidence](verification-v2.md#browser-scenarios) and [passing assertions](../../../output/playwright/hallkeeper-care-2026-09-07/final-scenarios.json). |
| S4 · Recover a service problem | A client reports a problem → it is clarified and owned → a specialist handles the work → a delay or unsuccessful first response has a visible next action → the client receives an update and the result is checked. | **Demonstrated** — S4 outcome in [browser evidence](verification-v2.md#browser-scenarios) and [passing assertions](../../../output/playwright/hallkeeper-care-2026-09-07/final-scenarios.json). |

## Completion record and honest limits

All fourteen requirements now have substantive design or demonstration evidence; all seven representative scenarios have passing browser outcomes. **Designed in detail** marks material represented through the complete workflow rather than a clickable integration. No demonstrated scenario is left unfinished. The current design deliverable is complete; field and production validation remain explicit below.

Design completion requires all requirements to have substantive evidence, all seven representative scenarios to have reviewed outcomes, and no unresolved defect that undermines the intended experience in the demonstrated flow. A minor future enhancement is not a blocker. A wrong-room plan, falsely accepted handover, misleading client update or lost work is a blocker until corrected or the demonstrable scope is honestly repaired.

Report completion as a researched, reviewed and browser-exercised design concept when that is what the evidence establishes. Do not call it a production system, give it an invented 100/100 score, state that every possible pain point is solved, or claim measured stress/productivity improvements. The original image preference is evidence for the visual direction; it does not automatically accept v2's finished appearance.

Remaining field questions include whether experienced staff recognise the proposed work, whether they prefer the language, whether helpers actually respond, whether less screen use and repeated entry result, and whether organisers feel better looked after. Validate those later through observation and trials with hallkeepers and organisers, using correct venue data and procedures. Keep that learning separate from the current design-delivery finish line.
