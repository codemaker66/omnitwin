In the heart of Glasgow’s Merchant City, Trades Hall is a stunning historic venue that adds a unique touch to any occasion. Whether you’re planning a small wedding or a large celebration for up to 250 guests, this venue offers the perfect backdrop.

More than just a beautiful venue, Trades Hall is the home of The Trades House of Glasgow, a charity that’s been part of the city since 1605. By hosting your event here, you’re not only creating lasting memories but also contributing to the community – since part of the proceeds from events goes to supporting local charitable causes.
