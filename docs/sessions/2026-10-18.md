## 12:10 — Production database outage, 08:19–12:10 BST (Neon free-plan quota)

From 07:19 to 11:09 UTC, Neon refused every query from the production API. `/health/ready` answered
503 `DB_UNREACHABLE`, while `/health/live` stayed 200. The Railway API log gives the cause: `Your account or
project has exceeded the quota. Upgrade your plan to increase limits.`

- **Why the quota ran out.** Neon's free plan allows 100 CU-hours per project per month and scales an idle
  compute to zero after five minutes. The request-escalation sweep (goal 19) queries `requests` every ~20 s,
  so the compute never slept, and October's allowance was used up on the 18th.
- **Fix.** Blake moved the Neon project to the Launch plan (pay as you go). `/health/ready` returned
  `{"status":"ok","migrations":{"applied":89,"local":89}}` at 12:10 BST, and the sweep has not failed since.
- **Why nobody noticed for four hours.** No uptime monitor or Sentry exists yet. The setup sitting's steps 4
  and 5 add them; the `/health/ready` keyword monitor is the one that catches this failure.
- **Side effect.** Railway's deploy of #109 (11:27 BST) failed during the outage because its pre-deploy
  migration could not reach the database, and the previous container kept serving. The next deploy
  (`36648997`, 12:11 BST) succeeded and carried #109 live.
- **Follow-up (goal 19 S7).** Let the sweep schedule itself from the next `escalation_due_at`, so the
  database can sleep when nothing is due.
