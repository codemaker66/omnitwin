## 23:40 — The Grand Hall's floor stops flashing black (T-652, room fixes S1)

Branch `claude/planner-room-fixes`, worktree `E:/claude/planner-room`, stacked on PR #135's `b2d657f0`.
Blake's screenshot of the live planner at 21:41 on 18 October showed black dashes across the floor
whenever the camera moved. Evidence, scripts and frames: `D:/claude/planner-polish-20261018/room/s1/`
(outside the repo; `frames/README.md` names each frame).

- **Cause.** The plinth under the hall was a near-black box whose top lay 1 mm under the oak floor. The
  scene pass's depth24 buffer (near 0.1, far 900 with the sky) leaves a few depth steps per millimetre at
  20-30 m, and float rounding on the two huge triangles is that size, so the plinth won the depth test at
  scattered pixels. TRAA averaged the losses away at rest and showed them in motion; tablets (FXAA) and
  phones (MSAA, no TRAA) showed them always.
- **Fix.** `hall-section.ts` builds the plinth as a ring round the floor (`buildPlinthRing`): its top lies at
  the floor's level only outside the floor, from the room's planes out to the walls' outer faces (it is also
  the floor of the door recesses and the firebox); its inner edge repeats the floor's own edge vertices,
  so no T-junction crack opens; a strip 2 cm down reaches 5 cm under the floor's edge (and 5 cm out under
  the ring's top) so a crack would show slab, not the void; outer faces drop 0.42 m; no bottom. It stays
  inside the city model's cut-out (|x| <= 11.2, |z| <= 5.95), whose caps share its level just beyond. Same
  mesh name and material.
- **Second fix, found in code and confirmed in the render.** three's `hash()` reads its seed as `u32`, so
  negative seeds all became 0: every row of the window half (z < -1.24 m) drew the same stagger and board
  length, and many boards one tone. `hall-floor.ts` raises every row and column index by
  `FLOOR_SEED_OFFSET` (4096) before hashing. Rendered and measured from close top-down frames
  (joints detected in the image, 97-98% precision and 95-97% recall against the shader's own arithmetic):
  the share of end joints within 1 cm of the next row's was 98.1% on the window side and 1.9% on the door
  side before, 1.9% and 2.8% after.
- **Measured (RTX 4090, dev server, `zfight-orbit.mjs`; orbits of 36 frames at 1.5 deg, and 4 deg, 72
  frames where noted; poses opening, far, steep and Blake's).** With the plinth painted magenta, magenta
  pixels inside the floor: before 332 (far) and 378 (steep) a frame over the orbit, up to 7,289 in one frame
  (desktop), and 931 and 993 on the phone profile; after **0 in every frame** of every run: desktop at DPR 1 and 1.5,
  the 4 deg orbit, near 0.03 (the stress), the tablet and phone profiles. Dark specks a frame on the floor
  (as built, against the plinth hidden): before, desktop far 51.2 (worst 803) against 7.6, steep 96.3 (2,166)
  against 20.2, phone far 237.9 (2,928) against 3.5; after, desktop far 7.8 against 8.2 and steep 16.6
  against 15.1, phone far 4.1 against 3.4 (72 frames: 8.9 against 8.4), tablet far 6.8 against 5.9 (72
  frames: 10.7 against 9.8). Where counts are a few pixels a frame, two runs that draw the floor alike (as
  built and magenta, the ring unseen in both) differ by up to 20%, so a +10% margin is inside the noise there;
  the cut walls ease in wall-clock time, so runs also catch them at different heights.
  The firebox floor in a Walk view at 1.2 m: 9,699 of 9,728 pixels are the ring's top, none background.
- **Checks.** `hall-section.test.ts` (new: nothing under the floor within 1.5 cm, the band to the outer
  faces covered at 5 cm, the floor's own edge vertices on the inner edge, inside the city's cut-out,
  windings), `hall-floor.test.ts` (seeds above zero and below 2^24 over the hall, every row its own stagger
  and length, both halves' joints alike), `hall-geometry`, `hall-views`; web typecheck, ESLint on the touched

- **Seen, not changed here.** A thin dashed line of light crosses the floor at x of about -4.3 m in the Plan
