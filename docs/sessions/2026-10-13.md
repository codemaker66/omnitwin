The work is on branch `claude/t656-ai-metering` (worktree `D:/claude/t650-brief/repo`), uncommitted. The [engineering note](../engineering/ai-usage-and-billing.md) has the detail.

- **The ledger.** An append-only `ai_usage_ledger`; triggers refuse update, delete and truncate. One row per call or refused call, holding:
  - the venue, the person, the feature, and the model asked for and the one that served;
  - the request id, the four token counts and the outcome (`ok`, `refusal`, `max_tokens`, `error` or `budget_blocked`);
  - the cost in integer micro-USD, priced per attempt from `usage.iterations` and recorded with price-table version `anthropic-2026-10-13`.

  It holds no prompt text, names or contact details. Each call also writes one structured log line with counts, money and ids only.
- **The allowance.**
  - `AI_VENUE_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` (UTC month; 0.01–100000; $50 when unset; an invalid value stops boot).
  - Each call reserves its worst case under a per-venue advisory lock: request bytes plus 4,096 framing tokens at the dearest input rate, plus 16,000 output tokens, plus a fallback attempt. That is about $0.80 for a draft and $1 for a brief.
  - A call that does not fit is a `budget_blocked` row and 429 `AI_BUDGET_REACHED`, and nothing is sent.
  - `AI_ASSISTANT_ENABLED` is still the global switch.
  - The composers show their existing "not available now" wording. The draft panel shows its existing failure line.
