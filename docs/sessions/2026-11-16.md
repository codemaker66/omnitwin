## The Diary holds the whole book: a design, checked and approved (T-703)

Written by the Venviewer 2027 programme's lead, who held this design lane.

### What was made

The design for piece A of the platform plan:
[2026-11-14-the-diary-holds-the-book-design.md](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-14-the-diary-holds-the-book-design.md).
It covers the old diary's future bookings and holds brought in, a regular hirer's term, rooms sold together,
set-up and breakdown time, and holds that lapse. Seven build stages; four change the database.

### How it was made

1. Four readers gathered the facts on 14 November: the booking model and its one clash rule; how an import must
   meet the event bind, the People Book and its neighbours; the Diary's screens, the plan's promises and its
   critiques; and outside facts on exports, series and combined rooms.
2. A designer wrote it from a read of master `7c3461e7`.
3. An independent builder's check, with the code open, found five wrong facts, seventeen things that would not
   have worked as written, and ten contradictions. The worst: an import that could not resume after stopping
   half-way; re-runs that would miss cancellations and duplicate bookings; parts of a combined room left
   unlocked; new deadlocks; a twelve-night term sending twelve identical reminders.
4. A reviser answered every item. Where the check itself was wrong, the code decided.

### What Blake decided (16 November, by button, word for word)

| Asked | His answer |
|---|---|
| Do you approve this design? | "Approve it (Recommended)" |
| May a seller place a provisional hold? | "No, keep it to the office" |
| When should the reminder trial run happen? | "On the real holds (Recommended)" |
| When may a room's dates show on the public page? | "After thirty Saturdays (Recommended)" |

The questions were put to him in the merge coordinator's question form, with the design in front of him. His
answer on sellers leaves his 10 October decision as it stands. One decision in the design had not been put to
him then: what the public page shows for a room that is part of a booked combination. Added on 18 November
2026: he answered it that day, by button, "Booked, or Held (Recommended)". A part shows booked or held whenever
the whole combination is.

### What this entry does not claim

- Nothing is built. No product code exists for this piece.
- The venue's present system and its export have not been seen. The import, the thirty Saturdays, reminders
  being switched on, public dates and the plan's "done when" all wait for that. Lapsed holds, rooms sold
  together, set-up time and terms do not.
- The venue's own rules (which rooms combine, how long a hold lasts, set-up times, the office's words) are
  questions for the venue, each with a default that changes nothing until answered.
- The release lead reviewed the design on 16 November and passed it with no blocker. The revision that answers
  its findings has not been reviewed again.
