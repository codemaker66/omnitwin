## The first-open event question, #253 (planner-qol, T-686; its PRs say T-674)

Overnight, every mutant of the earlier rounds ran again on the pushed head b2d55e29 (06:00 to 06:05 UTC, three

The release lead's review of b2d55e29 found every held item fixed, with tests that fail on 2d2c9d26, and held two
minors (V5, V6) and two optional nits (V7, V8). One head answers them, on master 11541cd2. Its merge, de3d354f, had
one conflict again, the 13 November log, where #422's section and #253's were both appended: master's text is kept
whole, and #253's section follows it.

- **An answer this tab sent without seeing it land is its own (V5).** V1 let a reopen keep an unsent answer only over
  what the tab knew. So the tab's own earlier write, if it reached the layout and lost its answer on the way back, or
  was still on its way, read as an answer given elsewhere, and the person's newest pick was dropped.
  - The record now keeps, for each layout, the answers it has sent without seeing them land, and a reopen keeps the
    unsent answer over any of them.
  - That set starts afresh when one of the tab's own writes lands (the layout then holds that answer, after anything
    sent before it), and when a layout with nothing left to write is answered anew.
- **An answer held for someone no longer signed in is not shown to the next person (V6).** The answer A gave stayed
  on screen for B as "Kept for this visit only", and B's reopen of the same layout applied it again.
  - From its first write, the record watches who is signed in. Once someone else is, the open layout's unsent or
    this-visit answer gives way to what the layout holds, so neither B's view nor B's reopen brings it back.
  - Nobody signed in is no change of person: the auth bridge passes through nobody while it checks anyone's access,
    the same person's included, so A's own access check keeps A's answer.
  - An answer whose turn comes with someone else or nobody signed in is still dropped before the network. It now
    gives way in the same way, rather than being held for this visit.
- **Words (V7, V8).** The comments, the design and the 15 November log say that every write is last-writer-wins, with
  the layout open or not, and that a reopen compares answers, not versions: one changed and changed back elsewhere
  reads as unchanged. The three claims of the 15 November log that the review named are corrected in place, marked
  as corrected today. V8 is answered in words rather than code: a tab left open across a release that adds a kind
  keeps it on the device, but writes it to a layout as a skip when it carries it there.
- **Not changed: V6's inferred part.** For about one render after Clerk moves to a new person, the auth store still
  names the last one, while the token getter already reads the new session. A retry in that moment could pass the
  person check and go out with the new person's token. Closing it needs the write to carry the person it is for and
  the API to refuse a mismatch: an API change, for its own pull request.

### The review of ba9185a2

The release lead found V5 and V6 fixed for the orders reviewed, with CI green on that head (16 of 16), and held four
minors (V10 to V13) and two nits (V14, V16); V15 was refuted. One head answers them.

- **A change of person drops the last person's unsent answers (V11), and catches one whose write is on its way
  (V10).** The watcher only hid an answer held for the last person, so in the order A, B, then A again, A's answer was
  written while the tab showed the layout unanswered. And an answer whose first write was still on its way read as
  kept, so the watcher passed over it, and that write's refusal or failure then held it for B.
  - Once someone else signs in, the last person's unsent answers are dropped, even if that person comes back, as the
    design already said. Wherever the open layout showed one of them, its write perhaps still on its way, or an
    answer held for this visit, it gives way to what the layout holds, and a write refused or failed after that
    marks nothing.
  - A write let go of on its way can still land, since it went out while its person was signed in. The layout then
    holds its answer, and the open layout shows it.
- **The writes sent and not seen landing are kept per write (V12).** A write refused for good leaves the count, even
  while a newer answer waits; a write seen landing clears it; and it starts afresh only when the layout is read with
  nothing of this tab's unsent or on its way. A new answer no longer resets it, so an answer dropped unseen at its
  turn, with nobody signed in, still counts when the layout opens holding it.
- **Tests pin the clearing of the receipt and of a change in hand (V13),** beside the in-flight and A, B, A orders.
- **In words (V14, V16).** The design says every write sent counts, even one sent while the browser was offline, or
  refused with a status that is tried again. The token lag left from V6 is now named on the T-686 row: the PATCH
  naming the person it is for, and the API refusing a mismatch.
