## 16:00 — The Grand Hall's walls in two-millimetre detail where you look (T-652, room fixes S4b)

Branch `claude/planner-room-fixes`, after S4a (`d26c6bda`) and a merge of `origin/master` (`96ef1b12`:
the night street, the planner chrome's slices 1-5, the vite pin). Goal: the deacon boards' names legible
and the portraits sharp up close, on computers and phones, by streaming a 2 mm layer over the walls'
atlas only where it is drawn large. Evidence, scripts and frames:
`D:/claude/planner-polish-20261018/room/s4b/` (`measure/final/`, `frames/`, `scripts/`) and
`room/renders/S4b/` (frames for Blake, named in its README).

- **Survey.** `project-detail.py` samples S4a's labels, stations, colour offsets and seam membrane at
  2 mm in the planner's own frame; `pack-detail.py` cuts 462 tiles of 512 x 512 texels (0.992 m inside
  an 8-texel gutter), 8.8 MB in WebP q86 with `detail/index.json` (bytes and SHA-256 a tile). Built
  from the public 8K panoramas only (the E57 cube faces were not used). Two runs gave byte-identical
  arrays and tiles; the detail reduced to the atlas's 5.2 mm matches the published atlas to a mean of
  2.0-2.9 levels with no shift (0.0-0.3 mm). The plan's estimate was 16-28 MB; the tiles hold what the
  panoramas resolve. No tile is all glass at this pitch, so none is left out. `HALL_SURVEY_REVISION` 6;
  `build.sh` and the survey README carry the step.
- **Runtime** (`hall-detail.ts`, `hall-detail-decode.ts`, `hall-detail.worker.ts`; `hall-materials.ts`
  `wallTexel`, `GrandHallModel.tsx`, `hall-finish.ts`): an array texture of slots (64 computer, 32
  tablet, 16 phone, none on a software rasteriser) made when the first tile arrives, an index texture,
  a worker that decodes and builds four mip levels in linear light, an LRU; tiles chosen each frame
  (in view, facing, below the cut, 0.19 px/mm or more at their nearest point), fetched while the camera
  is still or slow, four at a time; at most two copied a frame, one once 4 ms has gone on copies; a
  200 ms cross-fade, instant under reduced motion; failed tiles retried after 2, 8 and 32 s. Changes to
  the earlier, interrupted attempt of this slice: the array is allocated only with the first tile (it
  was 90 MB from the first frame); the detail gives way to the atlas from the mip level where the two
  hold the same (1.37 on a computer, 2.37 on a phone), so a resident tile's far end never sits on its
  last mip; the fade mixes in a square-root space (in linear light the first steps of a gilt letter
  over dark wood carried most of the change); the decoded buffers go back to the worker (a 60 s walk
  had left 124 MB of buffers to the collector, with 35-60 ms pauses about every 20 s); the per-frame
  copy budget (one copy took up to 10 ms on the phone profile at 4x CPU slowdown).
- **Harness.** The capture script holds each pose from the start of the frame loop with the orbit
  controls off: S4a's poses, forced only inside the render, let the controls lift the eye-level camera
  to their polar limit in some frames, which the layer saw as fast movement and held its fetches.

## 17:30 — Renderer sharpening measured and not adopted (T-652, room fixes S4c)

Branch `claude/planner-room-fixes`, after S4b (`3c34a1e9`). Goal: with the walls' texture sharp (S4a,
S4b), remove the renderer's own softness on desktop, keeping a change only if it measures better: 2 OCR
points or 0.01 SSIM or 5% less edge blur, with overshoot on gilt and edges at most 5% and at most
0.2 ms of desktop frame time; phones unaffected. **No candidate passed, so the planner draws as before.**
The commit corrects `PlannerRenderPipeline.tsx`'s header, which said the photographs write a lower
occlusion weight (every surface writes 1; the panes and the street's light write 0). Evidence, scripts
and frames: `D:/claude/planner-polish-20261018/room/s4c/` (`measure/summary.json`, `frames/README.md`,
`scripts/`, `evidence/s4c-candidates-clean.patch` with every candidate's code) and `room/renders/S4c/`
(each candidate beside the shipped frame, for Blake).

- **Method.** The planner (`/plan?sky=1`, desktop profile, 2000 x 1166, DPR 1, RTX 4090) at Blake's
  boards and portrait poses, 20:41Z and 12:30Z. Each candidate was switched inside one page (a
  measurement-only knob, never committed) and drew the same frame-indexed sequence from a rebuilt
  pipeline on jitter step 0: 64 frames held, frames 7, 18 and 36 of a 0.35 m/s move along the wall
  (120, 300, 600 ms), then back, easing to a stop, and held. The still the planner shows is the 16th
  frame after the stop (its convergence count). S4b's suite scored every frame: OCR of the boards
  against the transcription, edge blur (equivalent Gaussian, mm on the wall), SSIM of the portrait
  against the reference composite and against the published detail at the frame's density. Two
  additions: an SSIM against an ideal supersampled image (the detail box-averaged over each pixel;
  S4b's screen model is a tent filter, softer than any of the frames, so it scores blur higher) and
  screen-space overshoot on clean step edges against the unchanged frame. A repeat of the unchanged
  sequence sets the noise: edge blur within 0.3%, SSIM within 0.005, OCR within 1.2 points per frame.

- **Candidate 1, the bias**, reads the 2 mm detail's finest level where a pixel spans more than a texel
  (the portrait at 3.2 m). At the boards (1.9 m, 1.7 mm a pixel) the detail is already magnified and
  nothing changes; -1 gives exactly what -0.5 gives. Fully converged (64 frames) the portrait is 5.7%
  and 6.7% sharper; at the still the planner shows, 3.8% at night and 5.0% by day.
- **Candidate 2.** 16 frames after a stop the boards are 3.9% blurrier than fully converged and the
  portrait 1.9-2.8%; 32 and 45 frames recover 2.3% and 3.5% of edge blur on the boards and change no
  reading. Cost: 16 or 29 more frames drawn after every stop (5.5 ms each in this harness).
- **Candidate 3, RCAS.** Its limiter holds it back on dark wood and dark canvas, where the boards'
  gilt letters and the portrait sit: even at full strength the boards' edges sharpen by 2% and their
  reading by at most 1.1 points. Every strength moves the image further from the photograph,
  including from the ideal supersampled image.
- **Candidate 4 (Catmull-Rom history), not built.** It changes only frames drawn in motion and the
  frames that follow them. Against every SSIM in the suite the fully converged still beats the frames
  in motion and the 16-frame still by 0.0014 at most (most score higher in motion), so a perfect
  history fetch, which could at best make them match the converged still, could not add the 0.01 SSIM
  its copy of three's `TRAANode` would have to earn.
- **Cost** (4 s orbits, base and candidate interleaved three times in one page): RCAS 0.3 and the bias
  change no frame interval by more than 0.05 ms (median 5.5 ms; the harness's frames are paced by the
  CPU, so a pass this cheap does not show).
- **Unchanged.** Profiles (`render-quality.ts`), the governor and its no-give-back rule. Phones have no
  temporal anti-aliasing and none of these candidates would have reached them; real phones were not
  measured.
- **Checks.** Typecheck, ESLint on the changed file and the web build pass (the commit is a comment).
  The candidates' code passed typecheck, `render-quality` and `hall-detail` tests and compiled in the
  browser before it was measured and set aside.
- **Process.** Six GPU sessions under `gpu-session.sh`, each at most 71 s; one page load timed out at
  60 s and passed on rerun. Port 5231 is stopped and the lock released.
