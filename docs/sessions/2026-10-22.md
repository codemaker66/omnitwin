# Session log — 22 October 2026

## 16:00 — The Grand Hall's walls in two-millimetre detail where you look (T-652, room fixes S4b)

Branch `claude/planner-room-fixes`, after S4a (`d26c6bda`) and a merge of `origin/master` (`96ef1b12`:
the night street, the planner chrome's slices 1-5, the vite pin). Goal: the deacon boards' names legible
and the portraits sharp up close, on computers and phones, by streaming a 2 mm layer over the walls'
atlas only where it is drawn large. Evidence, scripts and frames:
`D:/claude/planner-polish-20261018/room/s4b/` (`measure/final/`, `frames/`, `scripts/`) and
`room/renders/S4b/` (frames for Blake, named in its README).

- **Survey.** `project-detail.py` samples S4a's labels, stations, colour offsets and seam membrane at
  2 mm in the planner's own frame; `pack-detail.py` cuts 462 tiles of 512 x 512 texels (0.992 m inside
  an 8-texel gutter), 8.8 MB in WebP q86 with `detail/index.json` (bytes and SHA-256 a tile). Built
  from the public 8K panoramas only (the E57 cube faces were not used). Two runs gave byte-identical
  arrays and tiles; the detail reduced to the atlas's 5.2 mm matches the published atlas to a mean of
  2.0-2.9 levels with no shift (0.0-0.3 mm). The plan's estimate was 16-28 MB; the tiles hold what the
  panoramas resolve. No tile is all glass at this pitch, so none is left out. `HALL_SURVEY_REVISION` 6;
  `build.sh` and the survey README carry the step.
- **Runtime** (`hall-detail.ts`, `hall-detail-decode.ts`, `hall-detail.worker.ts`; `hall-materials.ts`
  `wallTexel`, `GrandHallModel.tsx`, `hall-finish.ts`): an array texture of slots (64 computer, 32
  tablet, 16 phone, none on a software rasteriser) made when the first tile arrives, an index texture,
  a worker that decodes and builds four mip levels in linear light, an LRU; tiles chosen each frame
  (in view, facing, below the cut, 0.19 px/mm or more at their nearest point), fetched while the camera
  is still or slow, four at a time; at most two copied a frame, one once 4 ms has gone on copies; a
  200 ms cross-fade, instant under reduced motion; failed tiles retried after 2, 8 and 32 s. Changes to
  the earlier, interrupted attempt of this slice: the array is allocated only with the first tile (it
  was 90 MB from the first frame); the detail gives way to the atlas from the mip level where the two
  hold the same (1.37 on a computer, 2.37 on a phone), so a resident tile's far end never sits on its
  last mip; the fade mixes in a square-root space (in linear light the first steps of a gilt letter
  over dark wood carried most of the change); the decoded buffers go back to the worker (a 60 s walk
  had left 124 MB of buffers to the collector, with 35-60 ms pauses about every 20 s); the per-frame
  copy budget (one copy took up to 10 ms on the phone profile at 4x CPU slowdown).
- **Harness.** The capture script holds each pose from the start of the frame loop with the orbit
  controls off: S4a's poses, forced only inside the render, let the controls lift the eye-level camera
  to their polar limit in some frames, which the layer saw as fast movement and held its fetches.
