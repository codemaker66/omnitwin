# 15 November 2026

## The other rooms as surveyed (T-662): 13 to 15 November

How it stood at the end of 15 November. "Seen" means on the RTX 4090 in headless Chrome over WebGPU; "proved" means compared array for array; nothing here is published or live.

### What Blake asked and chose

- 13 November: "we were building every other room in the building in the new planner redesign way, have you completed that?" It had not been completed: the work stopped on 20 October at the tools stage, with the Saloon measured and nothing in the planner.
- 13 November, by button: **"Both, 3D first"**: the live 3D rooms as planned on 18 October, the Saloon first; after each room is in, its photographic set for the redesigned planner (T-687) from the same data.
- The 18 October decisions stand: measured sizes for drawing and planning, published capacities kept; the Robert Adam Room one room on two levels; Saloon, Reception Room, Robert Adam Room, then the galleries.

### What was done

- **The build runs from a room config** (`tools/surveyed-rooms/build-room.py`): one config per room, every path the Grand Hall never used behind a named gate (the hall opens none), and one sub-step at a time (`--list`, `--only`, `--resume`), because a whole stage held the shared machine's heavy lock for 59 minutes on 13 November.
- **Walls by master's method.** Since October the Grand Hall's walls take each part from its sharpest photograph. The room build now does the same by reusing the hall's own scripts through small seams, not a second copy (the copied port of October is what went stale). Proved on real data, the whole of the hall's end wall, master's scripts against the room build: labels, the 5 mm and 2.5 mm photographs, the portraits' varnish veil and the 2 mm image, 23 files and 49 arrays, none different.
- **A deadlock in master's label step, found and fixed.** `label-views.py` runs four band threads, each calling a matrix product through BLAS, which ran four threads of its own: on this machine (numpy 2.4.2, OpenBLAS 0.3.31) it stalled or crashed in three runs of four, once holding the heavy lock for 18 hours. A stack dump put the stall in `project.depth_lookup`. BLAS is now held to one thread there, in `build.sh` as well as in the room build; the labels are identical either way (22 arrays on a region).
- **Two faults that only a second room could show**, both fixed with tests: a wall whose rendered tiles are one texel narrower than its rounded length (gate B23), and two texels of 868,824 that the renderer placed behind its own camera at floor level, which the labels' 1 mm check refused (gate B24; they are left out and recorded). After each, the hall's results were compared again and did not move.
- **The Saloon, built twice**: on 13 November by the old blend, and on 15 November by the sharp method (12 walls, 288 detail tiles of 2 mm). Its gilt boards read line by line in the second where the first doubled and smeared them. The build's own check of the stained glass against the scan found no disagreement.
- **Two more rooms measured** into room configs, each with its numbered scripts, a README and a second check by ray casting:
  - Reception Room: an L of 16 faces, 101.75 m². Production stores a 13.4 by 11.2 m rectangle of 150 m²; the venue's own plan agrees with the scan to centimetres. Its two columns are square (0.28 m); no partition track was found; the floor rises 3 to 5 cm towards the windows. **Its window widths are a lower bound** (1.62 m): drapes hide every edge.
  - Robert Adam Room: 64 faces, 164.5 m² (lower 109.0, upper 52.5), the upper floor 0.594 m above the lower in four risers. The venue's site gives 139.4 m²; production stores only the upper part. No vaults: flat panels between flat beams.
- **The web's room-driven path** ([#431](https://github.com/codemaker66/omnitwin/pull/431)): beside the Grand Hall's code and touching none of it, a room is drawn from the `survey.json` its build publishes. An independent review found nothing blocking and ten faults, all answered. Seen: the Saloon on a dev page (every wall strip on its wall, lettering unmirrored), and, on a stacked branch not yet in a pull request, the Saloon in the planner's own scene behind `?rooms=surveyed`, with the near wall and the ceiling stepping aside by the camera's side.

### Open

- **Ghost outlines in plain plaster above the Saloon's pediments** by the new wall method, which the blend did not have. Cause not found. It must be fixed behind a method revision, so the Grand Hall's output stays what it is, before the Saloon is published.
- The build refuses free-standing columns, a curved wall and more than one floor level, so the Reception Room and the Robert Adam Room cannot be built yet.
- The record the build writes is version 1; the web's reader takes version 2 (a room block, each wall's height and kind, the atlas's and the floor's file lists).
- The Saloon's edits are not made: two doors stood open in the scan, and a piano and a sideboard stand in it.
- The planner's opening view is too tight for a surveyed Saloon: it comes from the hand-drawn 11.9 by 6.9 m outline. The measured outline (12.19 by 7.47 m with three shallow bays) replaces it after #411, whose tests pin the hand-drawn one, with the stored outline brought along by migration.
- The galleries are not in the July capture. Their floor is at E57 Z 7.55 (T-697's section), not the 8.6 to 9.2 inferred in October.
- Not proved: the hall's other three walls and its full two-hour build end to end; the Saloon's labels and photographs were computed once.
- For Blake, at the venue: the width of one Reception Room window's glass by tape, or a photograph with the drapes drawn back.
