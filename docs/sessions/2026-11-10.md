# 10 November 2026: the Day Board in the desks' register (T-686)

Blake, on the Day Board: "The dayboars does not match the aesthetic we set in the other admin
pages, the designs that are made to ensure a mind is not daunted ... it should match the other
admin pages we built". He asked for concepts from the Sunburst image app, then the build.

## Concepts and the choice

- **Sunburst was out of reach from this cloud session.** It is in no repository or artifact here,
  no computer folder was connected, and attaching the repository it lives in was refused. So the
  concepts were rendered in code from the desks' real tokens, fonts and room photographs (the
  Enquiries desk's sage ground, ivory sheet, copper plane and forest panel), on the crowded
  Saturday at 14:20.
- **Three concepts, then a choice.**
  - A: the run of the day, as a time-ordered list.
  - B: the timeline set on the ivory sheet.
  - C: the rooms, one row each with its photograph, what is on now and next, and a bar of the day.
  - Blake chose C.

## Independent review (maximum effort) and what changed after it

No blockers. Every finding below was reproduced in Chromium or with the real helpers, and fixed
with a regression test where a unit test can hold it.

- **An opened slot vanished under a filter on narrow screens.** For example, Done under "Live"
  took its room off the list mid-tap. The open slot's room now stays whatever the filter says. A
  test fails on the old code.
- **The wall had no ring on a room's Next function.** Next carries its ring in short words
  ("1 waiting · 25 min"). A wall shorter than 820 px spares that ring, with the legend, the
  photographs and the reason a changeover is at risk, rather than clip a card. The wall shows no
  list of waiting requests (concept C): the plane counts them and the next action names the
  first.
- **Overlapping bookings read "Turn-round 0 min · needs 1 h".** This hit a first and second
  option, and a talk inside an exhibition. Bookings at once now have no gap and no changeover
  risk between them. A booking already under way beside the lead is "Also now", not "Next".
- **Touch targets.** Every control is at least 44 px, and so is every slot line on a touch
  screen.
- **Counts that disagreed.**
  - The plane now counts every request nobody has today, as the list and the sentence do.
  - The sentence counts functions, as the plane does.
  - The line over the rooms names them rather than counting.
  - Another day's risks are not "now".
- **Smaller fixes.**
  - The NOW plaque leans inward at either end, so a phone is never wider than its screen in the
    evening.
  - Keyboard focus stays on a slot that moves from Next to the lead.
  - A risk under Later reddens the room's edge.
  - "Done for the day." replaces "Nothing more today." on past days.
  - The wall's slots no longer sit inside paragraphs.
  - The phone's next action is pinned at the top again, as D7 asks.
  - A wall prints whole; seven or more rooms on a short wall keep each room's lead and bar.
  - A booking of 20 hours or more across two dates counts in days either side of a clock change.
- **Unchanged on purpose.**
  - The plane's single-choice `aria-pressed` stages, as the Enquiries and Proposals desks have
    them; they are now spoken "At risk, 2" as the desks speak theirs.
  - Print leaves out the open slot: "Print today" prints the day.
  - The kept wall case's title still says "dark register": the frame is forest, and renaming it
    would be a second restatement.
