## The hallkeeper sheet's Setup from and Ready by (T-696)

Blake chose, by the question form: "Setup from + Ready by (Recommended)". Branch `claude/sheet-ready-by`, from
master `535ac56c`, ahead of F. Logs, renders and the proof script: `D:/claude/hallkeeper-sheet-alternatives-20261106/ready-by/`.

- **The bug.** Every surface printed `timing.setupBy` as the finish line: the PDF's "Ready by" and the day's "Room
  ready", the phone card, the print line and the Day Board's line. Both of that field's sources start a window (the
  room's earliest phase before the booking, or the booking start less the changeover time), so the crowded day's
  Grand Hall breakfast (Setup phase 07:00, doors 08:00) said the room had to be ready at 07:00, and steps due between
  07:00 and 08:00 were called late.
- **The rule.** `sheetTimes()` in `@omnitwin/types`: Setup from is `setupBy`, guests in is the booking's start, Ready
  by is the planner's typed final-touches time, else guests in. Every surface reads it; the frozen snapshot's shape
  does not change, and `setupBy` keeps its name with its meaning documented at the schema.
- **What booking start means (durable, needs a ruling of its own).** The Diary board ("Doors in", setup segments before
  the booking), the Day Board (doors at the booking's start), the conflict engine and the crowded-day fixture all
  read a booking's start as doors. The planner's When ribbon (`when-ribbon-model.ts`, goal 19 S6) reads the booking as
  the hire window: it writes a key-less "Setup" phase from the booking's start and an "arrival" phase at doors, and
  with no phases it draws doors an hour after the start. This fix follows the Day Board and the Diary, as Blake's
  option named. For a booking whose inner handles were dragged on the ribbon, the sheet, like the Day Board and the
  Diary, still reads the setup start as guests in. Reconciling the ribbon with the rest (and so the price window it
  feeds) is a product decision for Blake.
- Rulings (a planner's final time after guests come in is kept and shown in brick on every surface; the day folds a
  room ready as guests come in into one line; the header's five measured facts) are in the spec's As built:
  [2026-11-06-hallkeeper-sheet-a-d-f-design.md](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-hallkeeper-sheet-a-d-f-design.md).
- Page counts hold (the wedding 3, the board meeting 2, a short sheet 1, the breakfast 1). The wedding's page 1 no
  longer has room to point to the planner's words, because its day gained "08:00 Setup starts"; they open page 2.
- Not run here: the two Playwright specs whose expectations changed (`hallkeeper.spec.ts`, `day-board.spec.ts`) and the
  Postgres suite whose summary expectation changed (`internal-demo-review-postgres.test.ts`); CI runs them.
