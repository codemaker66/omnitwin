# Session log — 24 October 2026

## T-673: the browser gate's three flaky cases, fixed at their cause (flaky-test lane)

Session orchestration's brief: make master's browser release gate trustworthy before the freeze by fixing three
flaky cases at their cause, each reproduced first and proven by repeated runs; no longer timeouts, no retries, no
weakening of `.github/gpu/reconcile-browser.mjs`. Evidence it collected: `D:/claude/gpu-queue/flake-runs.md`. The
20 October workflow in the "Venue platform comprehensive redesign" session had root-caused two of them but shipped
nothing; its mechanisms were taken as leads and checked again on this tree. Branch `claude/flaky-e2e-c5779d`; all
harnesses, logs and repro specs under `D:/claude/flaky-e2e/`.

**The tour's stage click** (`twin-tour.spec.ts`, "a click on the stage walks to the next viewpoint", CPU shard 4;
seven gate rejections from 21 Oct). The test clicked once the HUD label painted. The HUD is React DOM and paints in
the viewer's first commit; the R3F scene holding the click listener (`TravelControls`) mounts only after R3F has
measured its canvas. Reproduced by delaying ResizeObserver delivery 0-1500 ms per page: 25 of 30 runs failed with
CI's exact message, and in every failure the click landed while the canvas was still 300×150 and before the listener
attached. A second gap, found by logging: R3F drew its first frame before the effects ran, so the `?look=` pose
reached the camera after that frame and a click in between was aimed through the stale matrix down −Z. In the browser
that second gap is masked by R3F's own raycast against the nav-ring discs (their matrices are equally stale and line
up), so it is proven by a unit test instead.
Fix: `TravelControls` sets `data-walk-ready="<node>"` on the canvas in the same effect that attaches the listener,
withdrawn mid-hop and on unmount, and aims with `camera.updateMatrixWorld()` first; the spec waits for the attribute.
After: the same injection 30 of 30; first-frame hold 20 of 20; both together 19 of 20; natural starvation (4 pinned
CPUs, 4 busy threads) 29 of 30. Every remaining failure is this machine's `net::ERR_NO_BUFFER_SPACE` (memory was down
to 1.4 GB free under other sessions' load), never the click. Mutation: the product fix without the spec's wait fails
15 of 20; the new unit test fails without `updateMatrixWorld` and passes with it.

**The 404 title** (`front-door-routes.test.tsx`, master run 39374059163 attempt 1). NotFoundPage writes the title and
robots tag in an effect that React runs in its own task after the commit; on the lazy page's first visit findBy hands
control back on the DOM alone, so the test could read the front door's title. The 20 October harness, ported to this
tree (commit held 8 ms, 2 ms preemption after Testing Library's drain timer): 9 of 10 failed with CI's message; the
robots test alone failed 5 of 5. Fix: the test waits for what the effect writes. After: 19 of 20 and robots-alone 10
of 10; reverting only the title wait fails 5 of 5.

**The front-door screenshot** (`sspp-hardening.spec.ts`, "captures deterministic public room route screenshot",
master 7f415b2e, 13,706 bytes against the 15,000-byte floor). The hero and rail fade from opacity 0 over 620 ms once a
requestAnimationFrame adds `rooms--resolved`, and toBeVisible() counts opacity 0 as visible. With that frame held
0-1500 ms: 9 of 20 failed, every one at 14,336 bytes with the hero at opacity 0; the passes ranged from 22,991 to
527,637 bytes. Fix: wait for hero and rail at opacity 1 and for every in-frame image to decode. After: 20 of 20, every
capture 1,063,629 bytes.

Open, not fixed here: front-door-routes' `beforeAll` imports the router cold under Vitest's 10 s hook timeout and
timed out in 2 of 30 local runs, both while e2e batches loaded the machine; never seen in CI. The only quick fix is a
longer timeout, which this lane may not use.
