# 24 November 2026

## Venue figures (T-752): the Reception Room's size is no longer shown

### Why Blake was asked again

On 22 November Blake chose to keep the venue's figures for the Reception Room (13.4 × 11.2 m, 3.2 m high) on the public
pages. The reason given to him with that question was wrong: it said the planner drew the same rectangle. The second
review of #471 found that the planner draws the room from its own hand-made outline, an L of 103.7 m² inside a
9.60 × 12.00 m box, and that the building survey (an L of about 102 m²) agrees with the planner. The venue's figures
describe a room about half as large again as either. See the [22 November log](2026-11-22.md).

### Blake's decision (24 November, by button, with the corrected facts in the question)

**Show no size for the Reception Room for now.** Its capacities stay. No dimensions are shown until the venue confirms
them. The other two choices offered were to keep the venue's figures and to show the surveyed size.

### What changed

- `packages/types/src/trades-hall-venue-truth.ts`: the Reception Room has no entry in the record the public pages
  read. The venue's published size is kept in a second record, `TRADES_HALL_WITHHELD_ROOM_DIMENSIONS`, which no page
  reads, so the venue's own statement is not lost. A test holds that no room is in both.
- Every page already handled a room with no size, as the galleries had none until 22 November, so no page's code
  changed:
  - the `/fresh` page's room card shows the Reception Room with no dimensions block;
  - the tour's room panel shows its two capacities and no Dimensions or Ceiling stat;
  - the tour's room list gives "100 standing · 60 seated" and stops there.
- The crowded-day test fixture still builds its Reception Room at the published size, read from the second record. It
  is a fixture, not a page.
- The three `*-rooms` baseline images for the open room list were recorded again on the CI runner (record-baselines
  run 45500757350) and looked at. The desktop and mobile images now give the Reception Room as "100 standing ·
  60 seated". The landscape image is the same file as before: its short panel ends above that row.
- **The visual check does not see this change.** A first recording (run 45500187056) passed all three cases
  against the stored images and rewrote nothing, because one short figure in muted text is under the
  comparison's tolerance. The stored images would have kept the old line, so the three files were removed and
  recorded afresh. The text itself is held by the unit tests and the tour's browser test, not by the images.
- From the reviews of #471: the comment in `room-selector.ts` now claims only what the no-break spaces give (no break
  inside a floor); one comment and two test comments state the 11 November decision in plain words instead of quoting a
  button label; the comment in `hall-spec.ts` gives the rooms page's own figure for the Grand Hall beside the plan's.

### What still gives the Reception Room's published size

- The terms page says the room dimensions shown on the site are the venue's own published figures, except the Grand
  Hall's. That is still true of every dimension shown.
- Three places read the database's row for the room, 13.4 × 11.2 × 3.2 m, and still give it (the second and third
  were added to this list after the review of #479):
  - the API's venue read for AI assistants (`get_venue`), which also gives a floor area;
  - the public read of a venue's spaces (`packages/api/src/routes/spaces.ts`), which the app itself loads;
  - a client proposal's layout summary (`ProposalLayoutVisual.tsx`), a line for screen readers that gives the room's
    size from the stored outline. A proposal keeps the figure once a version of it is made.
  Changing that row is a change to production data and was not part of this work. Blake was told, and on 25 November
  chose to leave all three until the surveyed room ships. See the [25 November log](2026-11-25.md).
- `packages/types/src/space.ts` describes the room as 13.4 m × 11.2 m in a list that no page reads.

### Not done, and why

- The second review suggested joining each section's name to its figure with no-break spaces in the tour's room panel,
  as the room list does (DD3). Not done. Measured on a local build with the page's own fonts, in a 412 px wide
  window, the longest section line is 103 px in a 120 px column, and the narrowest two-column layout the stylesheet
  allows gives 114.5 px. The join would change what the panel prints, which is a change of its own to test and look
  at, and is not part of Blake's decision.
