## Theatre auto-fill keeps a rear walkway (#410)

Decision, Blake, 12 November 2026 (about 11:05 UTC), verbatim: "Keep a rear walkway (Recommended)". The option read: auto-fill leaves at least 1.2 m clear behind the last row, about what the old layout left, and theatre goes back to 20 rows and 280 seats.

Why it came up: #391 moved the planner's Grand Hall to the scanned 21.135 × 10.59 m, which let `planTheatreLayout` fit a 21st row (294 seats). It used the 0.7 m wall clearance as the limit for the back row's centre, so only 0.445 m was left between the last row's chair backs and the end wall (the old 21 × 10.5 m hall left 1.21 m).

The rule (`packages/web/src/lib/theatre-layout.ts`, `planTheatreFill` in `auto-fill.ts`):

- `rearWalkwayM` (default 1.2 m, in `THEATRE_SPACING`) is measured from the back edge of the last row's chairs to the back wall: the last row's centre stops `rearWalkwayM + seatDepthM / 2` short of the wall. Side clearance (0.7 m) and the other defaults are unchanged.
- It is strict, at least 1.2 m behind whichever chair is in the last row. Rows are planned for the first chair's depth (Burgess Turini, 0.58 m); if stock gives the last row a deeper chair (the checked banquet chair, 0.62 m), the rows are planned again for that depth, on the door-clear path as well. At recorded stock this costs no row in any room; the one case found is the Reception Room with 24 to 119 usable pink chairs, which goes from 10 rows (120 seats) to 9 (108).
- The stated basis now names it: "… 2.4 m clear at the front and 1.2 m behind the last row."

Theatre fill, full room, default chair, before (294 etc. as of master at c7176b45) and after, using each room's bounding box in `packages/web/src/data/room-geometries.ts`:

| Room | Size (m) | Rows before | Seats before | Rows after | Seats after |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grand Hall | 21.135 × 10.59 | 21 | 294 | 20 | 280 |
| Saloon | 13.1 × 6.9 | 12 | 96 | 11 | 88 |
| Reception Room | 9.6 × 12 | 10 | 120 | 10 | 120 |
| Robert Adam Room | 11.4 × 12.7 | 11 | 176 | 10 | 160 |

The Grand Hall with its doors kept clear goes from 280 to 268. This supersedes the 21st-row disclosure in the [11 November log](2026-11-11.md). No e2e or visual baseline captures a theatre fill; the change does not trigger the 4090 GPU gate.
