## T-635 — Draft a proposal's message with AI ("Use in proposal", X1)

Where an AI provider is configured, the proposal composer offers "Draft the
message with AI" under the message. `POST /proposals/:id/message-draft` builds
what the AI is told on the server, at the proposal's own venue: the event as
the venue holds it, the client's name, their enquiry's words and their latest
words on the proposal since it was last sent (a change they asked for that no
sent version has answered). A name holding an email or a phone number is left
out (a public enquiry sent without a name stores its email as the name), and
email addresses and phone numbers are taken out of the client's words, found
as people write them (tabs, wide spaces, dashes, brackets, country codes) while
dates, times, timetables, ranges and budgets stay as written. The prompt says
it is the venue's events team writing to the client, from those facts alone;
with none of them the route refuses (`NOTHING_TO_DRAFT_FROM`) rather than have
the AI invent an event. Only the venue's commercial team may ask, only while
the proposal can take a new version.

The draft is shown apart in heather (new house tokens from the research's
measured tone table, contrast-audited) with its glyph and the word "Draft",
saying what it was written from and that it is not checked. "Use as the
message" puts it in, marked until the booker presses "I have read it": Save
version and Save as template wait for that. AI words that come back into the
message (an undo, a paste, a copy from a draft shown, even one put away, or
from an unread kept copy) are marked again; words the booker has read through,
and the version's own saved message, never are. Words written here and not
saved are kept to copy. Only the server's own "AI is off" hides AI for the
visit; a refusal for the proposal as it stands says so without a Try again
that could not work. The enquiry panel's "Proposal wording" AI panel is gone:
a proposal's message is drafted where it can be used. The heather card follows
F9's and F4's recommended defaults; Blake has not answered F9.
