## The quiz learns to deliberate, to remember, to hand you your year, and to count

Blake's brief, verbatim: *"go for it if you are confident you are making a
genuinely impressive, intelligent, fun for both people who have never heard
of trade's house and people who have been coming to craft dinners for
decades, all in a voice that is identical to ursula le guins."* The format
research (`docs/trades-house/quiz-authoring/FORMAT-RESEARCH-2026-08-16.md`)
had recommended keeping the answering format and adding four things. All four
shipped in this session; the spec's new section records the architecture.

### What was built

- **The Deliberation** — when the twelve leave the top two within 0.15, one
  more scene written for the pair (twelve authored, else composed from
  fourteen world lines), weighted to decide. He announces it himself as the
  aside on the twelfth reply; the button stays "Go on".
- **The town remembers** — twenty-five asides keyed on an earlier answer,
  spoken later, never grading the answer about to be given.
- **Your Year** — a ledger line per answer; a plate beside the reveal on wide
  screens, a sheet with a scrim on phones; share where there is a share sheet,
  copy where there is not.
- **The record** — `POST /public/quiz-runs`, no PII by construction, DNT/GPC
  honoured, text/plain keepalive send; migration 0062. **Not yet deployed:**
  Blake runs `pnpm db:migrate` then `railway up`.

### The review, and what it caught

An eight-agent adversarial workflow (voice editor, lore, TypeScript, security,
UX, three blind legibility readers). Every blocker and every "should" was
applied — 45 voice findings, 7 security, 9 UX, 8 TypeScript, 4 lore.

Three lessons worth more than the fixes:

1. **The prose gates check legibility, length and no-names — not taste.** The
   editor found four moralising epigrams and a dozen tautology/pun/Latinate
   tics in copy that had passed every automated gate. Her rules are now in the
   spec.
2. **Verify, don't assume, at the network edge.** The `trustProxy: true`
   forgery claim was tested from outside: `x-ratelimit-remaining` counted
   99→94 straight through forged `X-Forwarded-For` values — Railway's edge
   overwrites the header. Recorded at the config comment so it is not
   re-raised.
3. **A double cast is a smell with a design answer.** The route test needed
   `as unknown as Database["insert"]`; the honest fix was a one-method
   `QuizRunStore` port with a Drizzle implementation, and the test needs no
   cast at all.

### The bug the screenshots found

Headless Playwright screenshots (still the only way to *see* a render here)
showed the bubble retyping the scene from the first word the moment an
answer was given. Cause: the voice player owns one audio element; an aside
started while the scene was still being spoken reset the clock the scene's
typewriter was reading. `speakAside` now settles the scene first. That in
turn made the e2e measure the fully-spoken bubble at every reply — which it
had never done, because Playwright answers mid-typewriter — and exposed a
real 25px overflow on a 375×667 phone at scene nine. On short phones the
replied bubble is now capped at four lines under a fade and scrolls.
