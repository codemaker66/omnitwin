## What X's rebuild of x.com suggests for us (T-685)

Blake, sharing X Engineering's "Rebuilding the global town square from scratch" (4 Nov 2026): "is there anything
they have said that could give us ideas on how to improve our own performance in any capacity/area". The answer, its
evidence and the ranked ideas are in `D:/claude/x-perf-20261105/REPORT.md`: an eight-area audit of origin/master
f47fdbe6 with adversarial verifiers, plus live checks of venviewer.com and api.venviewer.com.

- **The database no longer sleeps (Blake's choice, about $19 a month).**
  - Neon suspended the production compute (`ep-dawn-glitter-abx2ux6f`) after 5 idle minutes. The next visitor
    waited for it: the same public `GET /venues` took 2.83 s asleep and 0.18 s awake. Sentry's first-after-idle
    samples were 0.8-1.5 s, against 25-50 ms warm.
  - Set to never suspend (`suspend_timeout_seconds: -1`) at 10:30 UTC.
  - Afterwards the first request after a 95 s lull took 0.12 s and 0.22 s, with a fresh pool connection each time.
    So the cost was the compute waking, not the API's 60 s pool idle timeout. The first request after a compute
    restart (Neon's maintenance window, Sundays 03:00-04:00) can still take about 3 s.
