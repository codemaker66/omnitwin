# 11 November 2026

## Whole-repository audit (T-695): the stranded claims, a fresh read, and six fix lanes

Blake's request, verbatim: "Read this entire project and find everything that's broken, inconsistent, inefficient, or no longer needed. Fix what you're sure about and list the rest for me to review."

Base for everything below: `origin/master` `f7b383c6`, the v1.0 tag commit. Master was frozen and untagged all day, so nothing here is merged or deployed; the fixes are pull requests for the after-tag queue. All work was done in clean worktrees of that commit, never in the shared checkout.

### What was found before any code was read

Three earlier efforts held parts of one picture and had not met:

- The 24 October release audit made 194 claims.
- A 5 November batch re-checked them on master with two independent verdicts each and found 107 still present, 86 of them written up ready to fix. Its fix lane stopped after four pull requests (#349 to #352).
- The 8 November final review then recorded "125 findings never reconciled", unaware of that batch.

So the first job was to fix from the verified list, not to rediscover it.

### The fresh read

Eleven readers each read one slice in full, read-only: nine slices of the API (platform, schema and scripts, planner and layouts, enquiries and people, proposals and money, Diary and bookings, event day and sheet, requests and suppliers and rota, AI and public endpoints and assets) and two of the web app (shell and API client, stores). They reported 167 findings: 11 high, 47 medium, 109 low; 78 where the finder is sure of the fix. Each was traced end to end by its finder. None has had a second reader, so they are leads, not verdicts.

Not read by this pass: the 3D and planner rendering code, most dashboard and page components, the editor's components, the reconstruction packages and tools, and the foundry control code in the API.

### The old claims nobody had re-checked

Thirty-four claims had no 5 November verdict. Read against today's code: 20 fixed, 7 duplicates of others, 4 partly fixed (`supplier-requests-2`, `events-diary-3`, `editor-save-3`, `public-claims-7`), 3 still present (`db-retention-5`, `a11y-4`, and `people-privacy-4`, which the original audit had refuted).

### Fixes

Eighteen commits on six branches fix 22 of the old claims and two of the new findings. Every fix began with a test that failed on master. Each lane's packages typecheck clean and its changed files lint clean on Windows; CI is the authority.

### Limits

- No independent review agent ran on any lane. The session lead read the source changes; the release lead's review is the first second reader.
- Nothing was exercised in a browser. #371 adds visible elements to the client's event page and the supplier's page, and #370 changes how the hallkeeper sheet behaves on a phone.
- Twenty-six of the thirty-seven planned fix clusters never started, and the fresh read's findings are unverified. The register lists both.
- Cost, for sizing what is left: the fresh read's eleven readers used 5.68 million subagent tokens in about two hours. With the fix lanes' first two hours and two other sessions running, the account's week went from 62 % to 89 %. The run was paused at Blake's request at 10:06 UTC, resumed at his word, and finished by the lead without further agents.
