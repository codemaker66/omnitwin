## 02:00 — The bind, independent verification

- **Defect found and fixed: a deadlock between writers of one event.** `syncEventStatus` locked the event
  `FOR UPDATE`, but every booking written or moved onto an event holds `FOR KEY SHARE` on it through the
  foreign key, and the two conflict. Two coordinators adding rooms to one event at the same moment, or two
  bookings moved between two events in opposite directions, each held a key share and waited for the other's:
