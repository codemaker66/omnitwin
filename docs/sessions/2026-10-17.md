## The planner's first ten seconds on a phone (red-team item 8, part)

Branch `claude/planner-phone-20261017` (worktree `E:/claude/build-20261016/planner-phone`), stacked on the honest
numbers branch (PR #105). Integration check defect 4: on a phone, choosing a lens from More set it and showed
nothing, so a 375 px visitor never reached the published capacities or "Ask us for a quote".

- **Lens sheet.** On touch and narrow screens the lens's registered panel (`LENS_PANELS`) rises as a modal bottom
  sheet (`LensSheet.tsx`): grab handle, a 44 px Close, focus moved in and returned to More, Tab kept inside,
  Escape, a tap on the dimmed room, or a swipe down (distance or a flick) closes it. The sheet is the lens on
  these screens: it is open exactly while a lens with a panel is active, and closing returns to Design. Motion is
  the shared spring core with the sheet's own near-critical tuning (seated within 2 px at 0.40 s, no overshoot);
  under reduced motion it snaps, while a drag still follows the finger. It stands aside during a room timeline
  preview. Desktop keeps the right dock unchanged.
- **Schedule card.** A signed-out visitor with no event link no longer sees "Your event schedule · No event linked
  to this layout"; signed-in accounts keep it, and a visitor who came by an event link keeps "Sign in to view
  your schedule" (`showsClientEventSchedule`).
- **Checked in a browser** on a private stack (Postgres 55411, API 3461, Vite 5461, all stopped afterwards) at
  375 × 812 with a coarse pointer: Guests showed the published capacities, Costs "Ask us for a quote" (44 px),
  Share scrolled inside the sheet; a real drag on the handle, Escape and a scrim tap each closed it with focus
  back on More; no schedule card for the visitor; at 1280 × 800 the lens stayed in the right dock. The browser
  pane reported reduced motion and was hidden, so the spring was proven in unit tests with stepped frames, not
  watched on a device.
- **Left for the planner session's PR:** auto-fill asking for the guest count first and the one-tap fix for
  "Short N seats" (both touch `placement-store.ts`).
- **Verification fixes.** Two Playwright specs still expected the "Your event schedule" card for a signed-out

  `dvh`. Playwright itself was not run on this machine.
