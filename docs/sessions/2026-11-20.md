**Lessons kept.**

- A rule about what a photograph shows is judged on photographs. A synthetic wall proves geometry (which texels
  are seen, which are clear); it cannot say which of two views looks right, because its pictures have one exposure
  and exact poses.
- Two builds are compared as pairs, not as two rates: how many of the same texels turned worse, turned better and
  stayed, counted by patch as well as by texel, beside the same count on texels neither build moved.
- A verdict sent between sessions can be lost at an account switch: #452's sat undelivered for a day. After any
  switch, ask the reviewer for the state of every open pull request.
- Before a branch's long checks, test that it still merges with master and whether master's lockfile or shared
  types moved.
- A patch's feathered seam must lie on patched content or on content like it: where the original is unlike (an open
  doorway beside dark wood), run the patch on under its neighbour.
