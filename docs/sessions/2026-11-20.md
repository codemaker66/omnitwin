**The review of #452 did not pass at `ff7dcf62`, and its findings are answered.** Two had to land.

- **The margin's fallback could print the rim it removes (SB1).** With an occluder margin (gate B25), a texel that
  none of its block's six ranked stations saw clear took the first ranked station that saw it at all, though a
  station outside the six had seen it clear; a Saloon wall has up to 27 stations. Now every station that saw the
  texel clear is asked first, in the block's own order, and a doubtful view is given only where stations saw the
  texel and none clear: the one whose ray passes farthest from the occluder's outline. On the review's synthetic
  wall the texels whose label's photograph shows a box are 51, against 99 without the margin and 211 as reviewed,
  and no doubtful texel has a clear view from any station. On the Saloon's twelve walls, built that night with the
  code as reviewed, 1,367 of 41,787 doubtful texels had a clear view from a station outside the six (most on
  window-rear-end, rear, front, fireplace and fireplace-rear-end).
- **The record's check before publishing was looser than the reader (SC1).** A swapped day and month in
  `source.captured` passed every step and was refused only by the planner. `record_faults` now holds every field to
  the reader's own type, and the config checker refuses an impossible date before any build. Against the reader's
  own parser, 85 of 86 test records now get the same verdict (50 before); the one left is the year 0000.
- **Two rulings on the review's wording, each on evidence.** The floor's resolution (SC2): the build's check refuses
  exactly the floors publish would refuse, not every resolution of 100 texels per metre or less, because the
  small-room suite's own room publishes validly at 60. The junction band (SB2): documented as a known cost of the
  margin, not redesigned, because the Saloon's 50 mm was measured with the margin as defined.
- **With a margin set, 1,550 Saloon texels that the six-station ranking alone leaves unlabelled now take a view**
  (740 clear, 810 doubtful), which follows from the two rules above. Without a margin nothing changes.

**Lessons kept.**

- A verdict sent between sessions can be lost at an account switch: #452's sat undelivered for a day. After any
  switch, ask the reviewer for the state of every open pull request.
- Before a branch's long checks, test that it still merges with master and whether master's lockfile or shared
  types moved.
- A patch's feathered seam must lie on patched content or on content like it: where the original is unlike (an open
  doorway beside dark wood), run the patch on under its neighbour.
