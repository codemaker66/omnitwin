- **Built.** Under the real sky the wall photographs are cut where the atlas marks glass (alpha below
  0.75; window boards kept; alpha to coverage on a phone's multisampled pass). `hall-glazing.ts` (new): a

  room, at single glazing's Fresnel (7.7% head-on) over 97% transmission, premultiplied, writing no depth
