## 20:39 — The planner's new chrome, slice 3: sound, the speaker and the Popover (T-671)

Blake approved the final chrome design on 20 Oct ("Build this design"); it is built slice by slice from
`D:/claude/planner-polish-20261018/ui/final/slices.md` behind `?chrome=v2`. Slices 1, 2 and 4 are PRs #150, #151 and
#155. Slice 3 renders the thirteen sounds from their recipes in TypeScript (`lib/planner-sound-render.ts`), so the
tests measure the samples that play, and adds the speaker (`SoundControl.tsx`) and a shared `Popover.tsx`.

- **The limiter added 6.6 dB to every sound.** A DynamicsCompressorNode applies makeup gain to everything below its
  threshold: the inverse of its curve's gain at 0 dBFS, to the power 0.6, which is +6.60 dB for spec 9's limiter
  (threshold −12, ratio 12). Chromium 147 measures 6.5999 dB. A trim after the limiter takes it back
  (`limiterMakeupDb`), and every sound rendered through the real chain in Chromium now peaks at its palette level
  (set-down −20.00, chair −24.00 and so on). A new compressor also holds a sound down for its first 125 ms (−6.8 dB at
  the start), and its look-ahead delays every sound by 6.04 ms, inside spec 9's "0–20 ms late"; the device's own
  output latency comes on top and is slice 12's to measure on a real phone.
- **The palette as specified breaks its own rules.** Measured with numpy/scipy on Chromium's renders (loudest 100 ms):
  the touch sounds spread 18.7 dB A-weighted (19.5 dB K-weighted) against "within 6 dB", and Send sits 8.1 dB over the
  loudest touch sound against "at most 6". The table set-down (103 Hz), Present (110 Hz) and the horizon (147 Hz) put
  their strongest energy below the rule's 150 Hz, where laptop speakers play little. The code keeps the table's levels,
  which its own test asserts. The smallest change that meets the rule moves seven sounds (Send −11.0 dB, measure
  −8.9 dB, act −4.7 dB, lift +3.9 dB, hour +2.1 dB, present −0.6 dB, refuse −0.4 dB). Both versions go to Blake's ear
  test with D1.
- **Sound ships Off** (`PLANNER_SOUND_DEFAULT_MODE`), as decision D1 directs until Blake answers. Spec 9's "one quiet
  offer" for an Off default waits on that answer.
- **Popovers sit at the top of the page.** The first frames showed the speaker's panel painting under the Details
  panel, because a panel inside its bar cannot rise above a later panel. Popovers now render through a portal, 8 px
  off the bar that holds their key, on a solid base so nothing beneath shows through. They never trap Tab.
