- **Cause (windows investigation, 18 October).** The glass was a photograph that the shader scaled by the

## 06:30 — The Grand Hall's cut follows its mouldings, in the room's light (T-652, room fixes S3b)

Branch `claude/planner-room-fixes` on `fa1288ee` (S3a). Goal: the section reads as a model maker's cut
through the real walls, its room edge following the dado rail, doorcases, boards, pilasters and
chimneypiece, lit by the chandeliers most at the room's side and by the sun or moon, with a gilt line and
a soft shadow under every cut that hold still on phones. Evidence, scripts and frames:
`D:/claude/planner-polish-20261018/room/s3b/` (`frames/README.md` names each frame).

- **Design changed from the brief, with evidence.** The brief capped the relief's section with its back
  faces. Ray-marched through the shipped relief (`scripts/backface_reach.py`, S3a's frames): from the
  opening and steep views (the camera beyond the cut wall) back faces fill 100% of the section; from
  Plan, looking down from over the room, rays inside the relief travel toward the wall and pass into
  the body, so back faces fill 2.8-21.9% of it per wall (S3a's Plan frame showed the night sky through
  those gaps). Built instead: the section itself. The relief is a height field, so its outline at a cut
  is a profile, sliced on the CPU (`ReliefSlicer`, a bucket index by height; 0.04-0.16 ms a wall) when
  a cut moves a millimetre, kept in a half-float row texture, and a strip of quads in each wall's cut
  plane is clipped to it in the shader: flat, facing up, true to the relief from every side. Its
  normal is up because it is up, so ambient occlusion sees a flat top with no MRT override.
- **Also built.** `aOpening` on every hall vertex (the window or door whose clear outline holds the
  triangle's centroid; S3c uses it). The gilt band and shadow on the walls' photographs under the cut
  (upright faces only: on ledges an 8 mm band of height spread into a smear and from above showed as a
  broken sliver), and the gilt line on the cut's room edge, which the brief's band alone could not
  give the views from beyond a cut wall (the room faces look away from them). The body's top goes
  where the relief at the cut lies behind the wall's plane (the end wall at 5.97 m in the opening view
  sat 3.6 cm proud of its panelling). Lines box-filtered; the gilt line never under 1.5 px (the
  brief's half-width rule); its distance measured square to the section's edge with a smoothed slope
  (measured square to the wall, it thinned to nothing on a pilaster's side). Light falls off from the
  wall's plane, not the relief's surface (from the surface it stepped behind every pilaster). Corner
  blocks light by their distance behind the nearer wall (a seam showed against the walls in Plan).
  Gilt at twice its measured average: at the average it read as the oak floor beyond it.
- **Measured (RTX 4090, dev server, `scripts/s3b-shots.mjs`, `scripts/s3b_measure.py`).**
