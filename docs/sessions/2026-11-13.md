# 13 November 2026

## People-privacy-5: erasure and the 24-month pass (#247, lane L1)

The privacy page promises erasure on request and the deletion of unbooked enquiries and contact records 24 months
after the last contact. #247 does both through one erasure (`services/people-erasure.ts`); the operator's page is
[erasure requests](../operations/erasure-requests.md).

### Decisions recorded

- **A contract or a deposit keeps the person (review finding B2).** A contract prepared or signed, or a deposit
  requested, on a proposal of theirs refuses the erasure with `PERSON_HAS_CONTRACT`. Contracts cannot be changed
  at all (migration 0104), and both are kept six years with the booking's records, as the privacy page's
  accounting rule says.
- **What is someone else's stays theirs.** An address another live person at the venue holds identifies nobody;
  a deal made from the person's enquiry that another live person leads is that person's; what the person wrote on
  someone else's proposal stays for that client, without their name. Only the erased person's full name and
  addresses leave such records.
- **Full names only.** A first or family name alone, and a full name another live person at the venue goes by,
  stay in kept text; the erasure page lists them under what stays by hand.

### Left by hand

- **The six-year pass.** People kept for a booking or a contract are listed by id (`kept` in the pass's result and
  in `POST /admin/retention/people`). Once the booking's financial year is six years past, the venue erases them by
  hand (erasure page, sections 2 to 4).
- The stores the erasure page names: accounts, `quotes`, `follow_up_tasks`, receipts not about an enquiry, and
  enquiry notices another enquiry could equally have written.

### The 2028 backlog

The People Book's backfill falls due together in 2028. A night acts on at most 100 people and 200 lone enquiries,
looks at no more than 500 of each and starts no step after 150 s (the cleanup job stops at five minutes), so the
backlog clears over several nights; the log says how many were left each night. After a restore from backup, the
recovery runbook repeats `POST /admin/cleanup` until nothing is deferred.

### Evidence

Disposable PostgreSQL only (a private cluster with every migration applied); see the PR for the runs and the
mutation checks.
