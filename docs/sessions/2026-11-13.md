## The visual layer review and the photographed hall's film toe (T-701): 13 November

Blake asked for a first-principles review of the whole visual layer. The review (ten reports, three sceptics, all outside the repository under `D:/claude/visual-layer-20261113/`) changed no code. He then asked why nothing in the codebase had improved, and this task is the answer.

### What was found

- **The live planner darkens its own photographs.** The Grand Hall draws the scan's photographs through Khronos PBR Neutral, which takes up to 0.04 from every channel to cancel a lit material's mirror reflection. A photograph has none. Simulated on `walls-4096.webp`: 11 levels less per channel on average, and the texels under L* 10 rise from 8.4 % to 14.9 %. The comment in `lib/capture-display.ts` and `docs/engineering/grand-hall-model.md` both said Neutral leaves the photographs as they are.
- **The fix belongs in the tone curve, not the material.** Exposure, white balance and the vignette sit between a material and the curve, so a correction in the material is exact in one mood only (up to 12 levels out in Candlelight).

### What changed

- `lib/film-toe.ts`: Neutral with the depth of that subtraction as one number, registered as the renderer's custom tone mapping by `CaptureToneMapping`. At 0.04 it is Neutral, value for value; at 0 a colour whose peak is under 0.8 leaves as it arrived.
- The default stays 0.04, so the first pull request changes no pixel. `?toe=0` to `?toe=0.04` in the page address draws with another toe.
- A reading of the diff at 17:20 UTC found one case where that did not hold. The grade's saturation (Evening: 1.02) takes a zero channel of a saturated colour under zero, and the first curve held such a channel at zero where Neutral runs its quadratic below zero and adds light: about one 8-bit level in gilding and brass. The curve now gives Neutral's answer there in proportion to the toe (all of it at 0.04, none at 0), takes its steps in three's own order and gets its curvature from the page (6.25 exactly at the default), so the default is Neutral for every colour. The 4090 gate had passed on the head before this (17:14 UTC); the fix went up before any review began, so the review and the browser look each happen once.
