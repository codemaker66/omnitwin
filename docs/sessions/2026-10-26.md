### Built
- All 44 catalogue pieces that stand in a room are drawn in code; the two cloths and the place setting are
  dressings and draw nothing of their own.
- Shadows: each piece casts the sun's map with closed boxes or an inset copy of its drape (a material the view
  discards and the shadow pass keeps); a faded or tinted ghost casts nothing. Contact shade under every foot.
  Baked occlusion by Lambert's polygon formula, the floor and cloth cavities. Lean detail on phones.
- The damask's figure was made truly mirror-symmetric so a quarter of its tile is painted and copied; with
  shape bounds and one figure for both maps its first use fell from 742 ms to 82 ms (tests prove the copy and the
  bounds exact). The lectern's desk was rebuilt to slope down to the speaker on a closed body.
