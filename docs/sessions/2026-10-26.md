## T-673: TwinPage's Dollhouse switch, a fourth flaky case (flaky-test lane)

Session orchestration's report: `TwinPage.test.tsx` › "switching to Dollhouse checks the segment and hides the
walk-only HUD" failed on master runs 40279751773 (50157f49) and 40284359345 (fc0b1d55, a CSS-only merge) with
`expected 'false' to be 'true'` on the Dollhouse radio's aria-checked inside the case's own 5 s waitFor; Test-job
reruns passed. Asked for: the cause, a small test-only PR, no longer timeout.

Cause, measured: the click is not dropped, it is overwritten. The walk (`useTwinWalk`) canonicalises `?node=` from an
effect that runs after the commit showing the stage, as a `replace` built from the address the page mounted with.
`findByTestId("twin-stage")` returns on the DOM alone; when the test resumed before that effect (the same
effect-after-commit gap as the 404 title on 24 Oct), the click pushed `?mode=dollhouse` and the pending effect then
replaced it with `?node=scan_000`. A harness logging every `setSearchParams` write (snapshot, result, replace) showed
exactly that order in every failure. Reproduced with the 24 Oct injection (2 ms preemption after Testing Library's
drain timer): 20 of 20 failed with CI's assertion, final address `?node=scan_000`; without injection 10 of 10 passed,
the walk's write landing before the click.

Fix, test-only: the test renders the router's query beside the page and waits for the walk's `node=` before switching.
Under the same injection: 20 of 20 pass, final address `?node=scan_000&mode=dollhouse`. The real file: 5 of 5 runs,
15 tests each; lint and typecheck clean. Harness and logs: `D:/claude/flaky-e2e/twinpage/`.

Found on the way, not fixed here (T-674): the same stale-address write is a product bug. `useTwinWalk`'s hop loop
keeps the `arriveAt` from the moment the hop began, and React Router 7 hands a functional `setSearchParams` updater
the snapshot from the render that created it, not the current address. Switching to Dollhouse during a glide (the
mode radios stay enabled mid-hop) is undone when the glide lands: measured `?node=scan_000&mode=dollhouse` becoming
`?node=scan_001` with the mode back to walk. `useTwinMode`'s comment that the functional form preserves the other
hook's key does not hold under React Router 7.

## T-674: the tour keeps its mode and viewpoint when their URL writes cross (flaky-test lane)

Session orchestration asked for the product fix as its own PR after #212. Measured first, with both hooks rendered
under a router and frames stepped by hand: a hop that lands after a mid-glide switch to Dollhouse drops `?mode=`, and
the dive (`useDive`'s `onArrive`: a teleport, then `setMode("walk")`, in one tick) lands back on the old viewpoint
(`?node=scan_000&mode=dollhouse` → dive to scan_002 → `?node=scan_000`, walk).

Cause: React Router 7's functional `setSearchParams` updater gets the params of the render that made the setter
(read in 7.18.2's `useSearchParams`), so any write from an older closure, or the second of two writes in one tick,
rebuilds the query from an address that has since changed. A fix that read the last committed render would not hold
in the browser either: the walk's per-frame progress renders outrank the router's transition, so a mode switch can stay
uncommitted until the hop lands. The router itself knows the current address at once: in the app's data router a
navigation on the tour's loaderless routes completes inside `navigate()` (checked in `handleLoaders`), and a
declarative router's history updates synchronously.

Fix: `useTwinSearchParams` (`packages/web/src/twin/twin-search-params.ts`) edits the query from the router's current
address (a data router's state, or its pending navigation; a declarative router's history), lets each edit decide push,
replace or no write from that address, and drops writes once the visitor has left the tour's page. Both hooks write
through it. An Opus review (xhigh) found no defect for the app's router and four ways to make it hold more widely, all
taken: decide `setMode` from the address (two quick presses mis-stacked history), the pathname guard (a landing hop
wrote onto the next page, on master too), pending navigations (routes with loaders), and tests that capture the hooks'
functions before the tick as `onArrive` does.

Evidence: `twin-url-writes.test.tsx`, 18 cases under a MemoryRouter, a data router and a data router with loaders,
all failing with master's hooks and all passing; twin unit tests 834 pass; typecheck and lint clean. The TwinPage mount
race #212 waits out also passes 20 of 20 without the wait under the same preemption. In a real browser (headless
Chromium under `browser-hold.sh`, the tour's mocked fixture), a switch to Dollhouse mid-glide was undone on landing
5 of 5 times with master's hooks and held 5 of 5 with the fix. Harness and logs: `D:/claude/flaky-e2e/t674/`.

## T-672: a Diary move reaches its event (L1, release audit batch A)

Release-audit findings events-diary-1, events-diary-3, people-privacy-2 and tenancy-f-1: moving a booking on the
Diary left its event behind. The event kept its old dates, which clients see and allergy-note retention counts from.
Its timed phases kept the old hour and room, which the Day Board and the sheet's set-up deadline read. An approved
sheet printed the hour it was submitted with.

Fix, in the move's own transaction:
- `syncEventStatus` (services/event-binding.ts) now also sets the event's dates from its live bookings: the confirmed
  bookings when it has any, else its options (`eventSpanFromBookings`). Every booking writer already calls it.
- `updateBookingLocked` and the proposed-window move call `movePhasesWithBooking`. It shifts the event's phases in
  the booking's room on the booking's day by the booking's own delta, in absolute time, so Glasgow wall-clock times
  hold across the 25 October clock change, and takes them to the booking's new room. Phases inside another live
  booking of the same event in that room stay.
- The phase editor now writes under the event lock that the move takes, so an edit can't undo a move.
- The approved sheet overlays live Diary timing on read, which fixes the sheet, its PDF and the Day Board. The stored
  snapshot is untouched.

Proof: `diary-move-reaches-event-postgres.test.ts`, 10 cases on the migrated disposable database.
