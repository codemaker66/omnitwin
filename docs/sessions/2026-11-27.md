# 27 November 2026

## T-672 follow-up: deleting a Clerk account takes the person's venue access away (cloud session "Audit A")

On 27 October the release audit's follow-up left one decision open: Clerk's `user.deleted` webhook unlinked the
user row and recorded the deleted account, but the row kept its role, venue and platform role. A new Clerk account
with the same verified email then linked that row again without an invitation, with the old access, and the row
stayed on the venue's team list and in its staff emails. Blake chose, on 27 November, that deletion also takes the
access away and that only a new invitation brings a venue role back.

- In the webhook's one transaction, under the account's lock: the row is unlinked and set to `client` with no
  venue and platform role `none`; its active workspace memberships become `removed`; the deletion record's metadata
  keeps what was taken away (role, venue, platform role) for whoever brings the person back.
- A new Clerk account with the same email signs in with no venue. A venue's new invitation grants the role it
  names, on that account. A platform administrator deleted this way loses platform access too, and is restored
  with `bootstrap-platform-admin`, which sets the row back by email.
- No migration: `client`, `none` and `removed` are already in their CHECKs.
- Open, not changed: layouts the person created stay theirs (`configurations.user_id`), as on any demotion, so a
  new account with the same email can still open them as their owner. A client's remembered notes are the
  17 October retention question.
