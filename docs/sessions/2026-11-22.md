**Decisions, with reasons.**

- **Blake, 12 November.** A supplier's contact and note are cleared one year after the booking ends, by the nightly clean-up; the supplier's name and the door times stay as the venue's record. Staff names on door marks are kept as the venue's safety record, also after an account is deleted: there is no erasure path for them, by design. The privacy page and the [engineering note](../engineering/supplier-arrivals.md) say both.
- **Anyone still in stays on the board** whatever becomes of the booking, so nobody locks up on them. Once the booking is off the Diary, undoing their arrival is refused: Gone, not erasure, ends their stay.
- **The history keeps the door, not the office.** Office adds and changes are not recorded, since each arrival already keeps its creator and revision, and a removal keeps only the account.
- **The late bound spares Gone.** Gone for someone already in is never refused for lateness, so a stay can always be closed.
- **Forms are kept, not the last read.** Keeping the calendar's last read would need the observations, arrivals and rota to hold theirs too, or a slot could briefly show the wrong state; a store of open forms holds no day's data, so no day can show another's.
- **An arrival is its own record per booking,** not fields on a supplier pass: a pass is what the supplier is sent, an arrival what the floor receives at the door. It is a default for Blake to overrule before merge.

**Limits left.**

- Arrivals are entered from the booking's panel, so only on days the booking is on the board; a pass and an arrival are not linked.
- Not built: a list of the day's arrivals by door, the contact as a phone link, an offline queue for taps (a tap's time is when the server received it), and a read of the door's history.
- A late boundary hour still blanks the board for one read. The open forms survive it, and a form now stays with its own day rather than following the board to another day of a long booking.
