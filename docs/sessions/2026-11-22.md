### #361 What changed (T-689)

**The release lead's review** of `8858a255`: 3 MUST FIX, 13 minors and a nit. All are fixed; minor 10 also leaves a limit.

- **MUST 1: a September function's folded move read "Room and times changed".** The fold recovered the room from its own words, and en-GB writes September as "Sept". A Diary move now keeps its places as data: where the floor last knew the booking, where the move put it, and its run of moves nobody has acknowledged. The fold compares room ids and instants, and names rooms by their current names.
- **MUST 2: a move back after hours was silent, and deleted bells that staff had read.** Silence is kept for a true Undo: the run began under two minutes ago and nobody has opened any of its bells. Otherwise the floor reads "Back to the original hours", "room" or "room and hours". Read rows are never deleted.
- **MUST 3: a change to the same event's booking or phase on another day was marked on this day,** and could be acknowledged from there for the whole floor. A change now marks only the day it concerns; since the follow-ups, the server places it.
- **Minors on the server.**
  - Got it and a fold are serialised, so a change is never both acknowledged and replaced (4).
  - Got it on a replaced move answers 409 `CHANGE_SUPERSEDED`, naming the newer change, and the board reads again after every outcome (5).
  - The read's cap is per event, not 500 rows across every event (6).
  - `proposeWindowCore` re-reads its hold `FOR UPDATE` and moves it from there, which also fixes #364's minor 21 (7).
  - The repeated hour on 25 October is judged by instant, and written "01:30 BST" or "01:30 GMT" (8).
  - A correction that ends in the past still retires the waiting moves (9).
  - A booking rebound to another event and moved in the same edit is told to its new event (10).
  - The board's read takes changes to the plan only; proposal traffic stays on the event-day board (15).
- **Minors on the board.**
  - The moved-off notice uses the panel's change list, and says "… has moved off this day." only when a waiting Diary move put the booking off the day; otherwise "… is no longer on this day." (11).
  - Its Got it has the panel's working state and "Got all N", and hands the keyboard to the day's sentence when the notice goes (12).

**The deep review** of `57481c08`, at max effort, confirmed every finding fixed, found no lock cycle between a fold and Got it and tenancy intact, and raised seven minors and two nits. All are fixed:

1. **The cap counted changes the board hides.** On a three-day conference, fifty newer phase edits on its other days could push Thursday's own move out of Thursday's read, and changes no board shows were dropped everywhere. The board now sends its day. Per event, the server keeps the changes placed on that day and those with no place of their own (an event edit, an enquiry's move, a phase with no room or time), marks the latter `eventWide`, keeps the newest 50 and says `truncated`. The board then says "More changes to this event are waiting than the board shows at once. The rest appear once the floor has these."
2. **A Got it answered "replaced" dropped the keyboard,** and its note went unheard. The panel, its working Got it and the keyboard now stay until the board has read again, and the live line stays mounted.
3. **A move into the past lost the place the floor last knew,** so the correction after it was told from the slip. The slip is kept as history told to no one (`move_told`, no bell), and the correction is told from the place the floor knew.
4. **"It changed again…" followed a quick Undo and outlived its change.** It is tied to the replacing change, and said only while that change is shown.
5. **One move across the clock change mixed two measures** ("starts 1 day later, ends 1 day 1 h later"). One measure now serves the whole move.
6. **A replaced bell one person had opened stayed unread for the rest of their role.** It is listed only for those who opened it, and never counted unread.
7. **The proposal's locked re-read did not check the event.** It now requires the event and the creator too; a hold rebound meanwhile answers 409 `WINDOW_DECIDED`, and nothing moves.

- **Nits.** The migration's header says its locks last until the whole migration batch commits, since drizzle applies every pending migration in one transaction; the pull request's lock-order sentence is corrected.
- **The wider checks found two more, fixed.** The read's new `NOT EXISTS` names its outer row through `outerColumn()`, as `outer-column-guard` requires, and the supplier-response notification suite's fixture declares `event_plan_changes`, which the bell list now reads.

**#406's minors, carried here.** The release lead passed #406 at `8a2a92be` and asked for its minors in this round. They come from a branch on that head:

- **F1: under way is decided on time,** before an exception lays its state over the slot, so a live slot held at risk stays "Also now".
- **F7/T1: the print guard** also catches zero opacity or font size, a transparent colour or text fill, `content-visibility: hidden`, and a zero height or width whose overflow is not visible; twelve such rules had passed the old guard. - **Nits.** Another day's count reads "Requests waiting this day" (it was "Waiting this day"); a line no longer repeats the reason the slot above it gives; the B6 note is indented to its label's words; "Also now" keeps one line on desks and the wall.

**Decisions, with reasons.**

- **Places as data, never read back from words.** Reading structure back from prose was the bug. A move row without places is never folded or guessed at: telling the floor once too often is safe.
- **A quiet Undo is two minutes from the run's first move, with no bell opened.** The Diary's Undo toast lasts 7 s, but Ctrl+Z works at any time; counting from the first move stops a run of small moves stretching the window.
- **A replaced move's unread bells go; opened ones stay with those who opened them.** An unread bell announces a place no longer true, and the newer change rings its own.
- **The cap follows the board's day and is said aloud,** because a silent cap hid a day's own change.
- **Proposal traffic stays on the event-day board,** where the hallkeeper already acknowledges it.
- **"Last acknowledged a change to this event"** words the line for the event, instead of tracking acknowledgements per slot.
- **Two of #406's nits are answered.** The rail's "not today" tag needs words true of a request on a booking released today as well as one on another day, and a new mark on the rail, so it is its own change. Phones keep the narrower label column for "Also now": a wider one wraps "LIVE · 30 min elapsed" on a 390 px phone.

**Limits left.**

- Not recorded yet: a booking released or cancelled (its slot leaves the board), or one added for today. No live frame announces a change; the board reads every minute and with each calendar refresh.
- A function that left a day is told there only on a board that showed it before.
- At most 50 waiting changes of an event on a board's day, newest first; a slot whose only changes are older carries no mark until the floor has the newer ones.
- The event a rebound booking leaves is not told, and a rebinding without a move tells neither. Both are rare (a merged enquiry), and as before.
- From the code, not run: while the web is live before the API, master's API answers the board's read with 400, and the board says "Couldn’t read what changed since …" until the API is live.
- After the stack, a small pull request attributes each Diary warning to the gap it describes: the release lead's ruling on #406. The wall switch's remount is left as it is.

**The deep review** of `35a2254e`, at max effort, found every finding fixed but minor 9, partly, and raised two more. Both are fixed:

**Decisions, with reasons.**

- **One frame, the next layout's room size,** because that is the sheet the crew holds, and a "Bring from store … · North wall" hint must match it. A planless side compares no zones rather than guess.
- **The doors, not the setup start.** Cutting at the setup start would pick a breakfast over a lunch that runs into the dinner's setup, and report 270 minutes instead of a short 0; a guard case pins it.
- **1,000 pieces a side and 250,000 candidate pairs.** Beyond either, the plan is not paired (`tooManyPieces`) and the counts stand. At 2,000 a side the worst bounded case still held the event loop for 544 ms, and the planner's own save caps a layout at 500 pieces. - **Which pieces have no front.** Tables, stages and barriers compare a turn modulo π, or π/2 when square; chairs, lecterns, AV, decor and bars face a way and keep the full turn.
- **Linen is never listed as staying:** a cloth is cleared and laid. That is a default about venue practice, which Blake can overrule.
