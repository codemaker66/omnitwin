## The API's Railway settings now live in Railway's new format (T-722): done

`railway.json` is gone from master, and Railway deploys the API from its own
stored settings, which `.railway/railway.ts` declares. The move was done in one
slot today, five days before the date we set ourselves and nine before
Railway's cutoff. The full record, with times, is
[the outcome](../operations/railway-config-move.md#the-outcome-22-november-2026).

### What happened

- Pull request 1 (#440) had merged on 20 November. Pull request 2 (#447) was
  brought up to date with master twice today; the release lead passed it after
  each merge, the second time against its own trial merge.
- The merge coordinator held every merge to master for the slot.
- A pinned plan from a clean checkout of master showed the same eight setting
  changes as every plan since 14 November, and nothing destructive.
- Blake said yes to the apply by button. The apply ran, Railway redeployed the
  API once with its migration step, and a second plan showed nothing left to
  change.
- #447 was then marked ready and merged. The deploy that merge started is the
  first without `railway.json`. Its own record names no config file, carries
  every setting, and its log shows the migration step before the start.
- The production receipt passed on the merge commit.

### Decisions made, with reasons

- **The apply waited for pull request 2's last check.** Blake's yes came while
  one check was still running. The checklist wants every check green before the
  production step, so the apply waited about fifteen minutes and the pre-flight
  was repeated. Applying first would have risked a longer spell with the
  settings in two places if that check had failed.
- **Blake looked at Railway's dashboard himself.** The checklist's first step
  is a look for pending staged changes. Railway's tool does not show them, and
  an agent should not sign in to the dashboard, so the look became part of his
  yes.
- **The plan and the apply ran from a clean, detached checkout of master**, not
  from a working branch, so the pinned plan's folder was exactly master's.

### What this entry does not claim

- Nothing checks, from now on, that Railway's stored settings still match
  `.railway/railway.ts`. Until something does, a change made on Railway's
  dashboard shows only on the next plan.
- The small things left over are listed in the outcome. None is fixed here.

## Venue figures against the building survey: the Robert Adam Room in two sections, the galleries' size recorded

The request came from the Trades Hall maquette lane (T-697). Its audit of its own heritage record, on 21 November,
compared the venue figures with the building survey and listed differences. The task: check the figures the product
shows, put each difference to Blake, and ship only what he confirms. Venue facts are the venue's and Blake's; nothing
was changed on the survey's word alone.

### What was found

- **The Grand Hall's dome note was already right.** The audit read a copy of the figures kept outside the repository
  that still said "a further 7 m under the dome". The product has not said that since 11 November (#391): the hall is
  21.14 × 10.59 m, 6.7 m to the coffered ceiling, with the dome as a remark that carries no figure. Nothing changed
  here. The venue's own page says "a magnificent seven-metre dome", which is where the old note's misreading began.
  The survey puts the coffer soffit at 6.70 m and the crown of the inner dome at 8.93 m above the hall floor.
- **The venue's rooms page, read again on 22 November,** against the product and the survey:

  | Room | Venue's page | Product before | Survey |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Saloon | 12 × 7 × 5.4 m | the same | 12.19 × 7.47 m inside the panelling |
  | Robert Adam Room | lower section 11.2 × 7.6 × 2.8 m; upper section 9.7 × 5.6 × 2.18 m | the upper section only, as the whole room | two levels, 0.59 m apart |
  | Reception Room | 13.4 × 11.2 × 3.2 m (150 m²) | the same; the area was never shown | an L of about 102 m², 12.47 × 9.69 m at its widest, ceiling 2.42 to 3.13 m |
  | North Gallery | 11.5 × 4 m, 2.1 m high | none; the record said the venue publishes none | main space 4.05 × 11.19 m, ceiling 2.81 m |
  | South Gallery | 11.5 × 4 m, no height | none | main space 4.06 × 11.03 m, ceiling 2.84 m |

### Blake's decisions (22 November, each chosen by button)

1. **Robert Adam Room: show both sections,** with the venue's own figures, on the front page's room card and in the
   tour's room panel.
2. **Reception Room: keep the venue's figures** until the surveyed room ships (T-662), so the public pages and the
   planner change together and never disagree. The planner still draws the 13.4 × 11.2 m rectangle.
3. **Galleries: record 11.5 × 4 m for each, with no height.** The page gives a height for the North Gallery only, does
   not say what it is measured to, and the survey's ceiling is higher.

The dome was not asked again: the 11 November decision stands.

### What changed

- `packages/types/src/trades-hall-venue-truth.ts`: a room's dimensions are now a list of one or more extents. A room
  given as a whole has one. The Robert Adam Room has two, named Lower and Upper, in the venue's order, and no figure for
  the two together, because the venue publishes none. The galleries have one extent each with no height.
- Front page room card (`pages/fresh/RoomDossier.tsx`): one line per extent. The Robert Adam Room reads "Lower section
  11.2 × 7.6 m · 2.8 m high" and "Upper section 9.7 × 5.6 m · 2.18 m high".
- Tour room panel (`twin/shell/RoomDossier.tsx`): a line per section under Dimensions and under Ceiling ("Lower 11.2 ×
  7.6 m", "Upper 9.7 × 5.6 m"; "Lower 2.8 m", "Upper 2.18 m"). On a 375 px phone the half-width column was 2 px too
  narrow and broke the line inside the figure, so a stat with section lines takes the full row where the stats are
  narrower than 240 px (a container query on the stats' own width).
- Tour room list (`twin/shell/room-selector.ts`): the Robert Adam Room's line gives both floors, "11.2 × 7.6 m and
  9.7 × 5.6 m", after the whole room's capacities.
- No page shows a gallery's dimensions today: the front page and the tour list four rooms. The record now holds them
  for the cards that will.
- The crowded-day test fixture writes one rectangle per room, so it takes a sectioned room's first section and its own
  stand-in height where none is stated.
