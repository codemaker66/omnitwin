**The deep review** of `57481c08`, at max effort, confirmed every finding fixed, found no lock cycle between a fold and Got it and tenancy intact, and raised seven minors and two nits. All are fixed:

1. **The cap counted changes the board hides.** On a three-day conference, fifty newer phase edits on its other days could push Thursday's own move out of Thursday's read, and changes no board shows were dropped everywhere. The board now sends its day. Per event, the server keeps the changes placed on that day and those with no place of their own (an event edit, an enquiry's move, a phase with no room or time), marks the latter `eventWide`, keeps the newest 50 and says `truncated`. The board then says "More changes to this event are waiting than the board shows at once. The rest appear once the floor has these."
2. **A Got it answered "replaced" dropped the keyboard,** and its note went unheard. The panel, its working Got it and the keyboard now stay until the board has read again, and the live line stays mounted.
3. **A move into the past lost the place the floor last knew,** so the correction after it was told from the slip. The slip is kept as history told to no one (`move_told`, no bell), and the correction is told from the place the floor knew.
4. **"It changed again…" followed a quick Undo and outlived its change.** It is tied to the replacing change, and said only while that change is shown.
5. **One move across the clock change mixed two measures** ("starts 1 day later, ends 1 day 1 h later"). One measure now serves the whole move.
6. **A replaced bell one person had opened stayed unread for the rest of their role.** It is listed only for those who opened it, and never counted unread.
7. **The proposal's locked re-read did not check the event.** It now requires the event and the creator too; a hold rebound meanwhile answers 409 `WINDOW_DECIDED`, and nothing moves.

**Decisions, with reasons.**

- **Places as data, never read back from words.** Reading structure back from prose was the bug. A move row without places is never folded or guessed at: telling the floor once too often is safe.
- **A quiet Undo is two minutes from the run's first move, with no bell opened.** The Diary's Undo toast lasts 7 s, but Ctrl+Z works at any time; counting from the first move stops a run of small moves stretching the window.
- **A replaced move's unread bells go; opened ones stay with those who opened them.** An unread bell announces a place no longer true, and the newer change rings its own.
- **The cap follows the board's day and is said aloud,** because a silent cap hid a day's own change.
- **Proposal traffic stays on the event-day board,** where the hallkeeper already acknowledges it.
- **"Last acknowledged a change to this event"** words the line for the event, instead of tracking acknowledgements per slot.
- **Two of #406's nits are answered.** The rail's "not today" tag needs words true of a request on a booking released today as well as one on another day, and a new mark on the rail, so it is its own change. Phones keep the narrower label column for "Also now": a wider one wraps "LIVE · 30 min elapsed" on a 390 px phone.

**Limits left.**
