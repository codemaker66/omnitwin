### A planner test that failed for one hour a day (`15e090fc`)

The full web suite failed once at 03:05 UTC, in `CockpitBottom.test.tsx`, which this work never
touched; it had passed at 02:45.
- **Cause.** With no date in the URL, the room timeline opens on the venue's operational day, which
  starts at 04:00 in the venue's zone (Europe/London). The dock's first anchor uses the browser's zone,
  which is UTC here and on CI. So from 03:00 to 04:00 UTC in British summer time, it opens on the day
  before, then moves to the venue's day and fetches again. The test looked for the phase cards before
  that second fetch landed.
- **Evidence.** It failed 6 of 6 at 03:05 UTC.
- **Fix.** The test now waits for the cards, and runs with the clock pinned at 02:30 and at 03:30 UTC.
  The 03:30 case fails with the old lookup.
- **Left alone.** A planner whose browser is in another zone briefly sees yesterday's timeline in that
  hour before it moves. This is cosmetic; the fix would be to anchor on the venue's zone from the start.
