# Session log — 11 October 2026

## 14:45 — T-652: a frame-rate governor for phones; an orbit no longer draws a selection box

- Blake, verbatim, on the planner: "60fps on the average phone someone in a major western city would have", using sael.net's work as the minimum; on 10 October, "Ryan Sael's has given us full permission to use his code and designs and ideas", and on this step, "you handle it, we have full permission, do it for me".
- Built: `RenderGovernor` gives up quality, a rung at a time, on a device that cannot hold its frame rate, after the governor in Ryan Sael's "Set the Mood" (1.6 s windows, warm-up, two slow windows a step, nothing given back during a visit). Adapted to a planner that draws on demand: it times the main view's draws, which `NativeCanvas`'s pacer can hold back behind the GPU's backlog (`lib/main-draw-clock.ts`); judges only frames the device paced (camera moving, loop running on, or a frame held back by the GPU); judges a window on its fastest 80% of frames; measures "slow" against the display's own rate, so a 30 Hz Low Power Mode phone keeps its quality; and reads what held the frames up from the main thread's frame work between draws. A CPU-held device gives up bloom and ambient occlusion and keeps its pixels; a GPU-held one gives up ambient occlusion, resolution to 1.5x, bloom, resolution to 1x, multisampling. Two steps in a row gaining under 8% end the stepping. The profiler's Copy report carries what was given up and measured. [Engineering note](../engineering/planner-rendering.md#holding-the-frame-rate).
- Found while measuring, and fixed: on a phone a one-finger orbit also drew a selection box from its second move (the touch gesture fell through to the mouse path once it passed the tap threshold), selecting the furniture it swept and re-rendering the furniture, dock and panels each frame. A CPU profile of a 6x-slowed orbit put 30% of the main thread in React; after the fix an orbit changes no store and draws 17-39 frames a second where it drew 9-24. A second finger no longer starts a box or a drag of its own either. Pinned by three touch tests that fail on the old code.
- Measured with the new `packages/web/scripts/planner-governor.mjs` (phone profile, 12 tables of 10, this PC, development server): full speed, nothing given up; CPU slowed 6x, it gave up bloom (nearly half the slowed render: frame work about 27 to 15 ms a draw) and stopped, 18-41 to 33-62 frames a second at 2x with multisampling kept; GPU held by another page's compute (about 29 frames a second, frame work 0.08 of the frame), it stepped resolution to 1.5x, neither step gained (the other page set the pace), and it stopped.
- Rejected on the way, by measurement: the render's own CPU time as the classifier (it fell below any fixed share once bloom went, though the thread stayed saturated) and the R3F frame-update rate as the frame rate (the pacer's held-back updates overstate a GPU-held phone's frames).
- Limits: no real phone measured; a phone filling its own pixels (where resolution steps gain) was not reproducible on the RTX 4090, so that path rests on the unit tests until Blake's phone report (`venviewer.com/plan?space=grand-hall&profiler=1`, Copy report). Blake has not yet seen the governor's steps on a device.
