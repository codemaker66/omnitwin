# Session log — 2026-08-22

## Nothing scrolls, on any screen, at any height

Blake, with a screenshot of the threshold at 100% zoom: *"at 100% zoom on
desktop you need to scroll to see everything: we must have no need to scroll
on any page on desktop or mobile, fix this"*.

He was right, and the guard that should have caught it had a hole in exactly
the shape of the bug: the no-scroll sweep ran at four iPhone sizes and nowhere
else. The threshold sized his portrait by WIDTH — `clamp(360px, 36vw, 560px)`
— and a width cannot know the viewport is 720px tall. Measured before the fix:
104px over at 1512x945, 195px at 1366x768, 198px at 1280x720.

### Method

A measurement harness (`packages/web/tmp/fit.mjs`, git-ignored) walks every
state — intro, threshold, twelve scenes with every option expanded, every
reply, the deliberation, the weighing, the reveal, the ledger open — at twenty
viewports, and reports the overflow per state. Three traps it taught:

1. **Measure what the test measures.** The harness first used
   `documentElement.scrollHeight` vs `innerHeight`; the e2e uses
   `max(documentElement, body)` vs `clientHeight`. The harness said clean while
   the suite went red.
2. **Measure the tallest state.** The threshold GROWS as his lines accumulate,
   and the bubble grows as it types. Sampling on arrival proves nothing.
3. **Measure the worst content.** The result stack varies by ~37px depending
   on which Craft it names. The Barbers and the Hammermen are the wordiest;
   a walk that happens to land on the Masons proves nothing.

### Found

- Both hero-painting screens sized a tall painting by width. They now cap it
  by the height available, with the gilt frame hugging the scaled image.
- The weighing screen hid `.convener-speech` — the SVG skin's bubble — but
  never `.convener-painting-speech`, the skin that actually ships. An empty
  bubble was holding 88px under a thinking face, which is both the whole of
  that screen's overflow and the exact thing the comment above the rule says
  must not happen. A rule written for one skin and never updated for the other.
- The result screen had a continuous height-compression system starting at
  1040px that stopped short of its two prose blocks and its tail of links, and
  no rule at all for 431-979px wide — so an iPad rendered at full tablet scale
  and stood 32px over. A wide-and-short screen wraps less prose than a
  narrow-and-short one, so the crest now keeps its size at ≥1200px rather than
  paying for someone else's wrapping.
- The smallest phones had 19px of headroom on the wordiest Craft — one wrapped
  line from scrolling, which is why that guard flickered instead of failing.

### Verified

Twenty viewports × every state, locally and then against production: no
scrolling anywhere. Headroom on the wordiest Craft is 18-42px where it was
8-38px or negative. The e2e sweep now runs at ten viewports rather than four,
the threshold has its own test (it costs 13s of beat timers, which is why it
cannot live inside a twelve-scene walk), and the assertion names the element
that overflowed. 26/26 e2e on three consecutive runs, 4593 web unit tests,
typecheck 0, lint clean, build ok.
