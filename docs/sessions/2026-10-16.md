## T-635: where an enquiry stands in the Diary

Built on `claude/t635-enquiry-day` from the [design record](../design/enquiry-day-answer-2026-10-13/README.md)
(revised after a six-lens critique on 13–14 October; its "As built" section records what the build settled).

- **API.** `GET /bookings?enquiryId=` returns one enquiry's live bookings (active, not deleted, in rooms the
  Diary still shows, soonest first), gated by `canReadDiary` at the enquiry's venue: 404 `ENQUIRY_NOT_FOUND`,
  403 `FORBIDDEN`, 400 for any other query. Proven on PostgreSQL by `enquiry-bookings-postgres.test.ts`,
  listed in the platform database gate. The approval email reads "Your enquiry for the Grand Hall at Trades
  Hall Glasgow on Friday 14 May 2027 has been approved." under "Enquiry approved", with no "Great news!".
- **The Diary's words** (`day-answer.ts`) now say an overdue decision as the Diary does ("decision was due
  Mon 5 Oct"), count another event's timed phase as using a room ("In use for …"), keep each line's kind and
  booking, and share the free-room count with Find.
- **The desk.** A fourth fact, In the Diary, held at two lines; an In the Diary section (Try again, Hold a
  date, Open this day, then the enquiry's own bookings elsewhere and the day room by room with published
  figures); Approve's sentence in a reserved slot, read again at once on Approve and changing at most once.
  The reads settle a quarter second and keep 32 answers. `diaryVenueOf` in `role-capabilities.ts` is the one
  rule for whose Diary Find and the desk read.
- **Checks.** Web typecheck and lint clean; 1,682 dashboard, Diary and guard unit tests; the email, route
  and Postgres suites; the four Enquiries browser specs locally on port 5221 (the new case on a desk and a
  phone: Approve holds its place from reading to a long answer to a failure), admitted to the reviewed
  inventory as admission 41 (393 cases).
- **Left for later:** the declined email still prints the ISO date and "We'd love to help"; the one-press
  hold and the reply joined to availability (#4 stays Partial).
