## T-627 — Native Three.js Gaussian rendering

Blake authorised implementation after the native/Spark baseline, followed by
visual inspection. Work is isolated in `codex/native-three-splats-20260918`,
based on production `ac338696d3e2b828a76d5c9a41c902640abba229`; the shared
checkout and Friday lane work remain preserved. T-627 supersedes the earlier
Spark renderer choice, not historical capture provenance or QA records.

The implementation removes `@sparkjsdev/spark` and its vendor patch. Three and
its types are pinned to r186; the first-party Gaussian addon renders one globally
sorted draw per scene. Cancellable native adapters retain SOG/PLY/SPZ/SPLAT/KSPLAT
assets, all SH bands, complete vendor levels, transforms and clipping. SOG WebP
decoding uses libwebp without browser colour/alpha conversion. A pinned Three
patch supplies the necessary lifecycle, orthographic, SH-direction, colour and
opacity hooks. A small Drei patch translates old update-range `offset` into
modern `start`, preserving instanced furniture under WebGPU. See
[the runtime note](../engineering/native-splats.md) for format, precision and
device limitations and [the baseline](../reports/native-splat-baseline-2026-09-18.md)
for the isolated comparison, which is not a product speedup claim.
