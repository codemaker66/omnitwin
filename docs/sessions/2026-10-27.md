### Adversarial review of #265 and #274 (deep-reviewer, max effort)
Each finding was run against the disposable database before it was acted on.
- Fixed in #265: an unchanged resubmit kept a version whose frozen name, guest count or style had since changed
  (the source hash is the room as set; a new version is now cut when the heading differs, from a draft too); the
  venue's second changes-requested or rejected email on an unchanged version was deduplicated away (keyed now by
  its history row); a decision judged on version 1 approved version 2 after a send-back loop (decisions carry the
  judged `snapshotId`; the desk sends it and reads the plan again after a refusal); an approve and a reject could
  deadlock on migration 0058's global lock and the loser answered 500 (now 409 `RETRY`).
- Fixed in #274: the seeded people of the live e2e stacks (no `clerk_id`, test-mode tokens) were treated as
  offboarded; a Diary screen reconnecting with a token issued before its Clerk account's deletion re-linked the row
  by email and stayed open for up to 12 hours (the webhook now records the deletion in `general_audit_log` with
  the unlink, and `getUserByClerkId` never links that account again).
- Not changed: REST edits checking the review lock only before the write (#260 fixes it); Diary command authority
  is read before the command's own transaction, not held in it (one round trip, as for HTTP).

### Found on the way, open
- The `/ws/configurations` socket's own save cannot commit on a migrated database:
  `configuration_layout_revisions_source_check` (0025, 0063) has never admitted `websocket_autosave`, so every
  flush that reaches the insert answers `AUTOSAVE_FAILED`. No first-party client sends those frames; the editor
  saves through the batch. Recommendation: retire the socket's writes (the engineering map already calls the
  batch the only writer); the alternative is a migration admitting the source. Recorded in
  [live co-editing](../engineering/live-coediting.md) by #274.
- Offboarding by deleting the Clerk account leaves the local row's role and venue. A new Clerk account with the
  same verified email links that row again without an invitation (pre-existing in `getUserByClerkId`), which
  matters if the Clerk instance lets anyone sign up. Recommendation: on `user.deleted`, also take the row's venue
  role away, so only a new invitation brings the person back.
- `POST /ops/handoff-packs/from-configuration/:configId` still admits the layout's owner (tenancy-f-2's optional
  extra): the planner's Ops lens calls it for their own layout and it writes a pack record, not the venue's day.
- The Reviews desk test "keeps the steps mounted, and waiting, while the same review is read again" failed once
  on #267's CI; #260's `861005aa` fixes it at its cause and is ported into #267.
