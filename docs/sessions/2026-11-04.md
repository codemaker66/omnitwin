## T-682: the snapshots on WebGPU with the sky on

The v1.0 release lead passed on the exterior session's probe
(`D:/claude/th-facade-20261028/perf/wgsl-probe.mjs`): on WebGPU with `?sky=1`,
seven or eight pipelines failed with an empty `struct OutputType { }`, and the
sky drew black.

Measured on the RTX 4090 (Chrome WebGPU, D3D11), master 5c5cee13; the probes,
reports and frames are outside the repository in `D:/claude/webgpu-target-safe-mrt/`.

- With the view's own frame loop, sky=1 compiles 158 shader modules with 0 WGSL
  errors at 12:00 and at 18:00, and the sky draws. The 23-25 failures appear
  only when the planner lab's `advance()` drives the frames: R3F's advance draws
  through NativeCanvas's precompile hold, with the job's target and MRT still
  set. The lab's advance now waits for the hold (`nativePrecompileSettled`), and
  driven so the probe reports 0.
- The failure that ships is in the snapshots, which draw into a target of their
  own whose one texture has no name. A material's own outputs
  (`material.mrtNode`: the panes, the street's light, the floor marks) keep none
  of their members there, three writes an empty output struct, and one refused
  pipeline voids the image. With the sky on, the plan sent with a layout (and
  with Submit for review) came back blank at 12:00 and at 18:00, and the
  current-view snapshot threw at 18:00 ("Native poster readback is blank").
  With the sky off both drew.
- The fix, `lib/target-safe-mrt.ts`: where a target names none of a material's
  outputs, they write their colour alone into its first texture. In the scene
  pass three merges them into its plain MRT as before, and the 158 modules the
  view draws are byte-identical to master's (SHA-256 per module, 12:00 and
  18:00). With the fix both snapshots draw with the sky on (12:00, 18:00) and
  off, with no failed pipeline (`snap/sheet-snapshots.png`).
- Tests: `lib/__tests__/target-safe-mrt.test.ts` drives r186's own
  `MRTNode.setup`: a plain `mrt` keeps 0 members in a target with no name or in
  PMREM's cube (`PMREM.cubeUv`), the target-safe one keeps 1, and a scene-pass
  target keeps all 3; every `material.mrtNode =` must use a target-safe maker.
  `native-precompile.test.ts` covers the settling.

Open: a snapshot taken while a precompile job holds the frames would still draw
with the job's MRT set (not seen; it could wait for `nativePrecompileSettled`
too). From the release lead's review of #331 (T-680): at low cuts the bay board
may share the section cap's plane for its first centimetre behind the wall
plane; a follow-up once #331 merges, with the cap in the coplanar test.
