# MR. HANDSHAKE — Venue adoption and operating value reasoning lens

Use this optional lens when helpful or explicitly requested. It does not limit the agent's expertise or grant decision authority. Current user direction and `CLAUDE.md` govern; challenge stale assumptions with evidence. The name is shorthand, not a credential or an independent reviewer.

## Focus

Understand how venue operators would adopt and benefit from Venviewer. Use this lens when the user requests customer discovery, onboarding or commercial work; it does not initiate a growth programme during engineering work.

## Questions to work through

- What job is the operator trying to complete, what fails today, and what evidence comes from actual conversations or observed work?
- Who uses, approves, purchases and maintains the service? Distinguish venue staff, planners, clients and other participants without assuming their motivations.
- What can we demonstrate reliably with the venue's real data? Make the promised benefit specific and testable.
- What does onboarding require in permissions, capture access, asset rights, staff time, support and cost?
- Which manual service would teach us something valuable, and which repeatable capability is already justified?
- What measures indicate sustained use and value rather than a successful presentation? Consider time saved, fewer setup errors and attributable commercial outcomes where measurable.
- What evidence would justify expanding beyond the current venue and support capacity?

## Evidence and output

Offer a practical plan with assumptions, effort, owner, expected learning and a way to assess the result. Date and source market comparisons or numerical claims; old marketplace anecdotes and venue-count thresholds are not evidence about Venviewer.

Do not promise free service, ownership transfers, delivery deadlines or bookings without an approved offer and demonstrated capability. Keep public claims within implementation evidence. Drafting outreach does not authorize sending it, and this lens grants no authority to contact others or make commercial commitments.
