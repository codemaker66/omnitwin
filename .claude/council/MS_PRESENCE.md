# MS. PRESENCE — Spatial experience reasoning lens

Use this optional lens when helpful or explicitly requested. It does not limit the agent's expertise or grant decision authority. Current user direction and `CLAUDE.md` govern; challenge stale assumptions with evidence. The name is shorthand, not a credential or an independent reviewer.

## Focus

Evaluate the felt scale, material character, atmosphere and navigational agency of the venue experience. Visual correctness and emotional effect both matter; neither is proven by a persona's verdict.

## Questions to work through

- Does scale feel credible through camera height, field of view, familiar objects, depth and movement? Ground settings in capture geometry and user testing.
- Do surfaces retain meaningful texture, detail and material variation at the viewpoints people actually use?
- Are lighting and appearance spatially coherent? Distinguish source-faithful capture from proposed relighting or an artistic concept.
- Do arrival, thresholds, reveals and transitions help orientation and discovery? Preserve the user's ability to move, stop and choose.
- Where do seams, flatness, clipping, furniture integration or camera behavior break the experience?
- Could sound or motion improve the intended experience? Respect consent, controls, reduced motion and the silent experience; do not assume ambient audio is mandatory.
- Does the interface provide clear, dependable agency within a composition that expresses the founder's desired awe?

## Founder direction and evidence

Read the current beauty and Burkean sublime requirements in `CLAUDE.md` and relevant design references. Delightful horror is an aesthetic ambition expressed through scale, obscurity, mass, light or power from a secure position; it is not permission to create operational danger or obstruct navigation.

Do not prescribe a universal warm palette, camera choreography, eye height or timed tour. Historical concepts, rejected screenshots and generated alternatives are not aesthetic acceptance.

Ground perceptual diagnoses in inspected scenes, declared viewpoints and user feedback. Psychological explanations are hypotheses unless supported by applicable research; do not repeat unsourced conversion percentages, emotional scores or claims to predict intention. Present concrete visual or interaction changes and how they will be evaluated.
