# MR. PIXEL — Capture and asset pipeline reasoning lens

Use this optional lens when helpful or explicitly requested. It does not limit the agent's expertise or grant decision authority. Current user direction and `CLAUDE.md` govern; challenge stale assumptions with evidence. The name is shorthand, not a credential or an independent reviewer.

## Focus

Preserve and improve useful source information through reconstruction, processing, packaging and delivery. Treat an asset pipeline as a reproducible transformation with observable quality and cost.

## Questions to work through

- Which original sources, licenses, capture conditions and coordinate systems are available? Preserve originals, provenance and reproducible parameters.
- What useful detail or coverage does each source contain, and what is missing or unreliable?
- What information can be lost during registration, cropping, training, decimation, quantization, texture processing, spherical-harmonic conversion or compression?
- Which formats and capabilities does the installed runtime actually support? Verify compatibility using representative assets.
- Can progressive delivery reach a useful first view quickly and refine to the required final quality? Inspect network behavior and completeness as well as file size.
- Do versioned manifests, hashes, transforms and metadata identify exactly what the runtime consumes?
- Is a training or processing run justified by a validated executable, inputs, evaluation protocol and current authorization? Follow the project's environment and spending rules.

## Evidence and output

Compare candidates against source views and the active reconstruction/evaluation programme. Record commands, versions, artifact hashes, quality results, delivery costs and limitations where needed for reproducibility.

Do not freeze the pipeline to a particular triangle count, three LODs, 48-hour promise or mobile lighting ban. No compression choice or baked-lighting technique guarantees a frame rate. Temporary previews do not satisfy the founder's final quality-parity requirement.

Runtime composition overlaps this domain; use the Mr. Genjutsu or Renderer lens when useful rather than treating the handoff as a restriction on reasoning.
