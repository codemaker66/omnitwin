Our starting point

One real venue.
A wider ambition.

Trades Hall Glasgow, Scotland

I’m Blake, the founder of Venviewer. I saw an opportunity to bring the visual and practical sides of venue planning closer together.

I’m funding the work myself and building the first version around Trades Hall in Glasgow. The aim is to make it useful for the venue’s team, then expand to other venues once it is ready.

The principle behind the product is simple: make everyone’s life easier, from the person booking an event to the people setting up the room.
